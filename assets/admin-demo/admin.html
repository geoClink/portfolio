Welcome to your dashboard

Here's the recommended setup order to get ready for your first client:

  1. Availability — set your weekly schedule and block any dates off
  2. Services — add the services you offer with pricing and duration
  3. Stylists — add your team members so clients can choose a stylist
  4. Settings → Stripe — connect your Stripe account to accept deposits
  5. Settings → Resend — connect Resend so confirmation emails go out
