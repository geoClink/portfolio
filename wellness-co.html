The Wellness Co.

A full-stack wellness platform for spas, yoga studios, and massage practices — service booking, practitioner profiles, membership tiers, and Stripe payments, all in one. Built as a Mindbody alternative without the $150/month price tag.

The Problem

Wellness businesses — spas, yoga studios, massage practices — are paying $100–200/month for Mindbody or Vagaro, platforms that were built for gyms and feel like it. The scheduling UX is clunky, the fees are high, and the branding is the platform's, not the business's. The goal was a custom-branded alternative that handles the whole workflow: discovery, booking, payment, and membership — owned by the business.

The Approach

Built on the same Supabase backend architecture as the Bakery Co., adapted for appointment-based businesses. Practitioners have individual profiles with their services and availability. Clients book a time slot, pay through Stripe, and receive a confirmation email via Resend. Membership tiers are managed through Stripe subscriptions — the database checks the membership status before unlocking member pricing. The admin dashboard lets the owner manage practitioners, services, and appointments without a developer.

The Outcome

A wellness platform that gives small practices the same feature set as Mindbody — service discovery, practitioner booking, Stripe payments, and memberships — at zero monthly software cost after handoff. Part of the CH Studios Small Business Platform Suite.
