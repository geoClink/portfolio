RateChecker

A SwiftUI iOS app that benchmarks your savings against live Treasury rates, forecasts where rates are headed with on-device Core ML, and shows exactly how much your current bank is costing you.

View on TestFlight GitHub
SwiftUI · SwiftData · Core ML · WidgetKit · FRED API · US Treasury API · 2026 · iOS

The Problem

The gap between a 0.5% APY savings account and a 5%+ HYSA on $10,000 is $450 per year — silently lost. Most savings apps show you what you have. RateChecker shows you what you're missing and gives you a clear path to better options.

The Approach

The app is split into two tabs: My Money (personal finance) and Rates (market data). My Money lets users add real savings accounts and see their blended APY, projected annual earnings, and the gap versus the current T-Bill benchmark. Rates shows live Treasury data pulled from the US Treasury Fiscal Data API — no API key required — plus a Core ML forecast that predicts T-Bill direction using 14 economic indicators entirely on-device.

A home screen WidgetKit widget keeps the live T-Bill rate always visible without opening the app. Background rate monitoring runs every 4 hours via BGTaskScheduler and fires a local notification when rates shift significantly. Every financial term in the app has an inline InfoButton that opens a plain-English definition sheet — keeping education in context rather than sending users to a separate glossary.

Sessions are persisted with SwiftData. The whole networking layer uses async/await with no Combine. The Core ML model runs on-device — zero server cost, works offline, no latency.

Screenshots and demo video coming soon.

The Outcome

RateChecker combines live API data, on-device ML, background monitoring, WidgetKit, and a contextual education pattern — all to surface a financial gap most people don't know they have.
