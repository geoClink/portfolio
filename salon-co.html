The Salon Co.

A 14-page marketing and booking site for a luxury hair salon — built as a direct alternative to Squarespace for salon owners who want something custom without the monthly fee.

Visit Live Site ↗
HTML · CSS · JavaScript · IntersectionObserver · Formspree · CH Studios LLC · Vercel

The Problem

Salon owners are paying $30–150/month for Squarespace or similar platforms that weren't designed for them — generic templates, limited customization, and a recurring fee that never goes away. The goal was a custom site that feels premium, loads fast, and is owned outright with no platform dependency.

The Approach

Fourteen pages of hand-written HTML, CSS, and JavaScript — no framework, no CMS. The scroll-based sticky header uses an IntersectionObserver to detect when the hero leaves the viewport. The reviews carousel auto-rotates with a CSS transition and pauses on hover. The gallery uses a filter system with CSS class toggling — no library needed. A mobile hamburger menu, FAQ accordion, and contact forms via Formspree round out the full site experience.

The Outcome

A luxury salon presence that competes with anything built on Squarespace — at zero monthly cost to the owner after handoff. Part of the CH Studios Small Business Platform Suite.
