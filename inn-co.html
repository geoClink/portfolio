The Inn Co.

A full-stack direct booking platform for a boutique hotel — room reservations, Stripe payments, self-serve cancellation, and a complete owner admin dashboard. Built to eliminate OTA commissions and give the owner full control over their bookings.

Visit Live Site ↗
Node.js · Express · Supabase · Stripe · Resend · CH Studios LLC · Vercel

The Problem

Boutique hotels and inns pay 15–25% commission on every booking made through Booking.com, Expedia, or Airbnb — platforms that own the guest relationship and push the property to the bottom of results whenever a larger hotel bids higher. Dedicated hotel software like Cloudbeds or Little Hotelier runs $150–400/month and is designed for large properties, not independent boutique owners. The goal was a direct booking platform the owner controls outright, with no per-booking commission and no ongoing platform fee.

Admin Dashboard

The owner manages every aspect of the property from a protected dashboard — room listings and descriptions, availability calendar with blocked date management, booking history, guest records, revenue reporting, review moderation, and direct Stripe and Resend account configuration from the UI. No code. No developer on call. Every change reflects on the live site immediately.

The Approach

The booking confirmation flow is webhook-driven — Stripe fires a webhook after payment clears, which writes the reservation to Supabase and triggers the confirmation email via Resend. A failed or abandoned payment never creates a booking record. Three email flows run through Resend: booking confirmation with stay details and a cancellation link, cancellation receipt with refund status, and contact form notifications routed directly to the owner.

Each reservation is issued a UUID cancel token at insert time. The confirmation email carries that token in a link — no guest account required, and the URL cannot be guessed or enumerated. When a guest clicks the link, the server checks the check-in date: cancellations made more than 48 hours before check-in receive a full refund processed automatically through the Stripe API. Cancellations within 48 hours fall under the hotel's standard policy — no refund is issued and the booking is marked cancelled in the admin.

The admin dashboard is protected by Supabase Auth with a rate-limited login endpoint and JWT validation on every protected route. The owner manages room availability, blocks dates, edits room descriptions, reviews incoming bookings, and configures their Stripe and Resend accounts directly from the UI — no code, no developer on call.

The architecture is multi-tenant from day one. A single middleware reads a TENANT_SLUG environment variable and scopes every database query to that property. Onboarding a second hotel means inserting a row and deploying a new Vercel project — no schema changes, no code forks.

The Outcome

A direct booking platform the owner controls outright — capturing bookings directly at zero commission versus 15–25% on OTA platforms. Guests book, pay, and self-serve their cancellations without any involvement from the owner. The multi-tenant architecture means the same platform can be white-labeled for additional properties as CH Studios grows the hospitality suite. Part of the CH Studios Small Business Platform Suite.
