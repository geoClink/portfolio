How I Work

I start by figuring out what "done" actually means before writing any code. Most problems that show up at launch were visible at kickoff — a missed edge case, an unclear requirement, a feature nobody asked for. I'd rather surface those in a conversation than debug them in production.

On client work I over-communicate. A short update before a client thinks to ask is always better than a long explanation after something breaks. Most "technical" problems are alignment problems in disguise.

I build with the user in front of me, not as an abstraction. Accessibility isn't a checklist — it's in the first prototype. Design should reduce friction, not add it. And the best implementation is the one the next developer doesn't have to untangle.
