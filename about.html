About Me

Full-stack web engineer based in Detroit.

My Story

I'm a Colorado State University graduate turned full-stack web engineer, currently in my second year at the MSU Apple Developer Academy in Detroit — selected for the advanced cohort of 25 from a first-year class of 250.

I build production web platforms for real clients: multi-tenant SaaS, webhook-driven payment flows, admin dashboards, and AI integrations. I've also shipped four apps to the App Store, which means I know what it takes to take a product from idea to production and keep it running.

Outside of work I'm usually on a bike, walking Detroit neighborhoods learning the architecture, or chasing down the next client.

What I Build

I build full-stack web platforms and SwiftUI iOS apps with an emphasis on accessibility, interaction design, and maintainable architecture.

Web and backend:

  • Full-stack Node.js/Express platforms with Supabase, PostgreSQL, Stripe, Twilio, and Resend
  • AI integrations using the Anthropic Claude API and on-device ML pipelines
  • Admin dashboards covering orders, inventory, analytics, CMS, and customer management
  • Payment flows including subscriptions, gift cards, deposits, and webhook-driven fulfillment

iOS:

  • SwiftUI app development with CoreML, SpriteKit, SwiftData, and WidgetKit
  • Cross-platform UX for iPad and macOS including full keyboard compatibility
  • Accessibility-first development with VoiceOver, Dynamic Type, and Live Activities
  • On-device ML inference with XGBoost and ONNX
