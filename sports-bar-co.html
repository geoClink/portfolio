The Sports Bar Co.

A full-stack reservations and events platform for sports bars — table booking, a live events calendar, and a party inquiry form. Built as a direct alternative to Toast for owners who want a custom web presence without the platform lock-in.

The Problem

Sports bars run on events — game days, watch parties, private buyouts — but most manage reservations by phone and promote events through Instagram stories that disappear after 24 hours. Toast and OpenTable charge hundreds per month and aren't built for the event-driven nature of sports bars. The goal was a platform that makes reservations and events first-class features, not afterthoughts.

The Approach

Built as part of the CH Studios Small Business Suite — same backend architecture as the Bakery Co., adapted for reservations and events. Table booking uses a date/time/party-size form that writes to Supabase and sends confirmation emails via Resend. The events calendar pulls from the database so the owner can add or cancel events without touching any code. Party and buyout inquiries go through a dedicated form that routes to the owner's inbox. The full site is owned outright — no monthly platform fee.

The Outcome

A sports bar web presence that handles reservations, promotes events, and captures private party leads — all in one place, at a fraction of what off-the-shelf reservation platforms charge. Part of the CH Studios Small Business Platform Suite.
