How it works

Explore. Go. Stamp.

Plan · Trips

Plan the trip, day by day.

Build a trip over several days with landmarks and your own saved places, then fly through it on the 3D map before you leave.

  • Reorder stops, see walking times and optimize the route in one tap
  • Add any restaurant, shop or venue from Apple Maps
  • Find hotels for each night with a hotel search for your dates
  • Trips sync with iCloud between iPhone and iPad

Every city page

Everything you need once you land.

Each city page answers "what now?", so you don't need five other apps.

  • Hotels

    Hotels on the map and a hotel search for your trip dates.

  • Places to eat

    Top eats near the landmarks, plus food and coffee on the map.

  • Events

    Upcoming concerts and games at landmark venues, with ticket links.

  • Weather

    The forecast for the city you're exploring.

  • Currency

    A quick converter for the local currency.

  • Airport

    The main airport, its code, and what makes it worth a look.

  • Say hello

    How to greet people in the local language.

  • Want to Visit

    Heart any landmark or Apple Maps place to save it for later.
