Planning the visit

  • Fly intoDes Moines International Airport (DSM)

    Des Moines International, south of Ames, is the main airport for central Iowa.

  • Getting aroundWalking / CyRide

    Central Campus is walkable. CyRide buses, run by the city, the university and student government, cover campus and all of Ames.

  • Colors and teams

    The school colors are cardinal and gold, and the teams are the Cyclones.

    Central Campus, Iowa State University, Ames, IA

    George E. Hallett designed this free-standing brick bell tower, built in 1897 to 1898, with Venetian Gothic towers as its model. It holds the Stanton Memorial Carillon and is a symbol of the university.

3 traditions to check off

Stamped! keeps these as a checklist, so you can tick each one off on the visit.

  • Don't Step on the Zodiac

    A bronze zodiac is set in the floor of Gold Star Hall in the Memorial Union. Legend says stepping on it means failing your next exam, so walk around it like generations of students.

  • Campaniling

    Tradition says you become a true Iowa Stater when you are kissed under the Campanile at midnight, and hundreds gather for "Mass Campaniling" at Homecoming. Visit the Campanile and listen for its bells.

  • Lake LaVerne

    Swans first came to Lake LaVerne in 1935 and were named Lancelot and Elaine. Legend says couples who walk silently around the lake three times are meant to be together.

History

Founded 1858

From Wikipedia

The Iowa State University of Science and Technology, also referred to as Iowa State University, is a public land-grant research university in Ames, Iowa, United States. Founded in 1858 as the Iowa Agricultural College and Model Farm, Iowa State became one of the nation's first designated land-grant institutions when the Iowa Legislature accepted the provisions of the 1862 Morrill Act on September 11, 1862. On July 4, 1959, the college was officially renamed Iowa State University of Science and Technology.
