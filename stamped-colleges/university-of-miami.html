Planning the visit

  • Fly intoMiami International Airport (MIA)

    Miami International, north of Coral Gables, is the main airport for the area.

  • Getting aroundWalking / Metrorail / Hurry 'Canes shuttle

    The campus is walkable. The free Hurry 'Canes Shuttle connects campus with Metrorail's University station.

  • Colors and teams

    The school colors are orange, green and white, and the teams are the Hurricanes.

    Lowe Art Museum

    Jerry Herman Ring Theatre

    Coral Gables, FL 33146

    Robert M. Little and Marion Manley designed this round theatre, completed in 1953. Its concrete roof is held by a steel ring, and it was built to withstand hurricane winds.

  4. 04

    Merrick Building

    5202 University Dr, Coral Gables, FL 33146

    Begun in 1926 in the Mediterranean Revival style, the university's first building stood half finished for more than 20 years after the hurricane and the end of the land boom. Robert Murray Little completed it in 1950 in the International Style around the original frame.

3 traditions to check off

Stamped! keeps these as a checklist, so you can tick each one off on the visit.

  • U Statue at the Rock

    The U statue on the Rock Plaza, beside the Shalala Student Center, is the classic campus photo spot. Visitors take a picture in front of it.

  • Homecoming Boat Burning

    Since 1956 a boat has been burned on Lake Osceola at Homecoming. Legend says that if the mast falls before the boat sinks, the Hurricanes will win the next day's game.

  • Bronze Ibis

    A bronze statue of Sebastian the Ibis, the university mascot, stands in front of the Newman Alumni Center. Visitors stop for a photo with it.
