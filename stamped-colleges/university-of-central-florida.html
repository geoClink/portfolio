University of Central Florida

UCF's main campus is laid out in rings: Gemini Boulevard, then Apollo, Mercury and Pegasus Circles. It is one of only two U.S. campuses with a concentric-circle plan, along with UC Irvine.

Planning the visit

  • Fly intoOrlando International Airport (MCO)

    Orlando International, southwest of campus, is the main airport for the region.

  • Getting aroundWalking / UCF Shuttles

    The circular core is walkable. UCF's off-campus shuttles link the main campus with more than 20 nearby apartment complexes, and students ride LYNX buses free with their UCF ID.

  • Colors and teams

    The school colors are black and gold, and the teams are the Knights.

4 buildings to stamp

  1. 01

    John C. Hitt Library

    University of Central Florida campus, Orlando, FL

    UCF's main library opened in 1968 at the center of campus, just south of the Student Union. The concrete building is an example of Brutalism and was named for the university's fourth president in 2011.

    Robinson Observatory

    University of Central Florida campus, Orlando, FL

    Built in 1995 for over $500,000, with Herbert Robinson paying almost half. The College of Sciences runs it and holds public viewings twice a month.

  3. 03

    Acrisure Bounce House

    4465 Knights Victory Way, Orlando, FL 32816

    360 Architecture designed this steel and brick-clad football stadium, built in 18 months and opened in 2007. It replaced the Citrus Bowl in downtown Orlando as the Knights' home.

    Addition Financial Arena

    12777 Gemini Blvd N, Orlando, FL

    HOK Sport designed this 252,000-square-foot arena, opened in 2007 as part of Knights Plaza. It seats over 9,400 for basketball.

1 tradition to check off

Stamped! keeps these as a checklist, so you can tick each one off on the visit.

  • Don't Step on the Seal

    Legend says you won't graduate if you walk across the Pegasus seal in the Student Union, though graduates may cross it once they have their diplomas. Find the seal and walk around it.

History

Founded 1963

From Wikipedia

With 70,674 students as of the fall 2025 semester, it has the second-largest on-campus student body of any public university in the United States. UCF is classified among "R1: Doctoral Universities – Very high research spending and doctorate production."
