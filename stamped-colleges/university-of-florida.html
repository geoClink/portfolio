Planning the visit

  • Fly intoGainesville Regional Airport (GNV)

    Gainesville Regional Airport, northeast of campus, has flights to major hubs.

  • Getting aroundWalking / RTS buses

    The historic district is walkable. Gainesville's RTS buses run frequent routes through campus.

  • Colors and teams

    The school colors are orange and blue, and the teams are the Gators.

5 buildings to stamp

  1. 01

    Century Tower

    Century Tower, University of Florida, Gainesville, FL 32611

    This 48-meter bell tower, begun in 1953, marks the centennial of UF's parent school and honors students who died in the World Wars. Its 61-bell carillon was completed in 2003.

  2. 02

    University Auditorium

    University Auditorium, University of Florida, Gainesville, FL 32611

    William Augustus Edwards designed this Collegiate Gothic hall, built between 1922 and 1924. Its cruciform interior carries a late 14th-century style hammerbeam roof.

    Built
    1924
    Architect

    Style

  3. 03

    Thomas Hall

    Murphree Area, University of Florida, Gainesville, FL 32612

    Built in 1905 and named for Gainesville mayor William Reuben Thomas, this Collegiate Gothic hall is one of the two oldest buildings on campus. It has long served as a residence hall.

    Built
    1905
    Architect
    William Augustus Edwards
    Style
    Collegiate Gothic
    Today
    Residence Hall
  4. 04

    Buckman Hall

    Murphree Area, University of Florida, Gainesville, FL 32612

    Opened in 1906 alongside Thomas Hall, Buckman Hall is one of the two original buildings of the Gainesville campus. William A. Edwards designed it in the Collegiate Gothic style.

    Built
    1906
    Architect
    William Augustus Edwards
    Style
    Collegiate Gothic
    Today
    Residence Hall
  5. 05

    Anderson Hall

    Anderson Hall, University of Florida, Gainesville, FL 32611

    Opened in 1913 as Language Hall, this Collegiate Gothic building by William Augustus Edwards now houses political science and religion. It was added to the National Register of Historic Places in 1979.

    1913
    Architect

  • Century Tower carillon

    The 61-bell carillon in Century Tower, completed in its present form in 2003, rings out over the center of campus. Stand on the lawn below to listen.

History

Founded 1853

From Wikipedia

The University of Florida is a public land-grant research university in Gainesville, Florida, United States. It is a senior member of the State University System of Florida. The university traces its origins to 1853 and has operated continuously on its Gainesville campus since September 1906.
