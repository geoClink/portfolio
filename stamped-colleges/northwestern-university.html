Planning the visit

  • Fly intoChicago O'Hare International Airport (ORD)

    O'Hare, west of Evanston, is the region's main airport. Chicago Midway also serves the city.

  • Getting aroundWalking / CTA Purple Line / Metra

    CTA Purple Line and Metra trains stop near campus, and the university's Intercampus shuttle links Evanston with the Chicago campus.

  • Colors and teams

    The school colors are purple and white, and the teams are the Wildcats.

4 buildings to stamp

  1. 01

    University Hall

    Northwestern University, Evanston, IL 60208

    Gurdon P. Randall designed this Victorian Gothic hall, begun in 1868 and finished in 1869. It is the oldest original building on campus and now houses the English department.

    Deering Library

    Northwestern University, Evanston, IL 60208

    James Gamble Rogers designed this Collegiate Gothic library, which opened in 1933 and served as the main library until 1970. It houses the University Archives and the Music Library.

  3. 03

    Pick-Staiger Concert Hall

    50 Arts Circle Dr, Evanston, IL 60208

    Edward D. Dart designed this concert hall of precast concrete and glass, completed in 1975. All 1,003 seats have a clear view of the stage.

  4. 04

    Dearborn Observatory

    Northwestern University, Evanston, IL 60208

    Cobb and Frost designed this limestone observatory in the Richardsonian Romanesque style, built in 1888–89. It was moved in 1939 to make room for the Technological Institute.

3 traditions to check off

Stamped! keeps these as a checklist, so you can tick each one off on the visit.

  • The Rock

    Students have painted the Rock since the 1940s, often guarding it for a day before painting it overnight. Stop by to see the latest message.

  • Weber Arch

    New students march in through Weber Arch at the start of their time at Northwestern, and seniors march out through it near the end. Walk through the arch from Sheridan Road onto campus.

  • Shakespeare Garden

    This quiet garden on the north side of campus is one of the stops on the admissions campus tour map. Visit it on a walk through campus.
