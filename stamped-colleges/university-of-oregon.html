Planning the visit

  • Fly intoEugene Airport (EUG)

    Eugene Airport, northwest of the city, is the nearest airport.

  • Getting aroundWalking / LTD EmX

    Lane Transit District's EmX bus rapid transit stops along the edge of campus, and students ride LTD buses with their ID.

  • Colors and teams

    The school colors are green and yellow, and the teams are the Ducks.

5 buildings to stamp

  1. 01

    University Hall

    University of Oregon, Eugene, OR 97403

    W. W. Piper designed this Italianate hall, built from 1873 to 1876 as the university's first building. Known for most of its history as Deady Hall, it is a National Historic Landmark with Villard Hall.

  2. 02

    Villard Hall

    University of Oregon, Eugene, OR 97403

    Completed in 1886, this Second Empire hall is the second-oldest building on campus. It was named for railroad magnate Henry Villard, the university's first benefactor.

    Built
    1886
    Architect
    Warren Heywood Williams
    Style

    Today

  3. 03

    Knight Library

    University of Oregon, Eugene, OR 97403

    Ellis F. Lawrence designed the main library, built in 1937, mixing Lombardic Romanesque and Art Deco. It holds more than 3 million volumes.

  4. 04

    Jordan Schnitzer Museum of Art

    1430 Johnson Ln, Eugene, OR 97403

    Ellis F. Lawrence designed this brick and cast stone museum, which opened in 1933 to house Gertrude Bass Warner's collection of Asian art. Its style mixes Romanesque and other influences.

    1930

  5. 05

    Johnson Hall

    University of Oregon, Eugene, OR 97403

    State Architect William C. Knighton designed this American Renaissance hall, built in 1914–15. It is the main administration building of the university.

2 traditions to check off

Stamped! keeps these as a checklist, so you can tick each one off on the visit.

  • Yell-O Duck Statue

    A 1,000-pound bronze of the Duck throwing the "O" stands between Matthew Knight Arena and the Ford Alumni Center. Throw the O beside it for a photo.

  • Bill Bowerman Statue

    A 2000 bronze of coach and Nike co-founder Bill Bowerman stands at the corner of Hayward Field. Visit it on a walk around the track stadium.
