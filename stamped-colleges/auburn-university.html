Planning the visit

  • Fly intoColumbus Airport (CSG)

    Columbus Airport in Georgia, southeast of Auburn, has Delta flights to Atlanta, home of the much larger Hartsfield–Jackson airport.

  • Getting aroundWalking / Tiger Transit

    The historic core around Samford Hall is walkable. Tiger Transit buses serve students, faculty and staff on weekdays during the semester.

  • Colors and teams

    The school colors are burnt orange and navy blue, and the teams are the Tigers.

    Samford Hall

    Samford Hall, Auburn University, Auburn, AL 36849

    Designed by Bruce and Morgan of Atlanta and completed in 1888, this four-story Italianate hall was modeled on the earlier Old Main. Its clock tower is the best-known symbol of Auburn.

  2. 02

    Langdon Hall

    College St, Auburn, AL 36849

    Built in the Greek Revival style in 1846 as the chapel of the Auburn Female College, it was moved to campus in 1883 and is the oldest building in Auburn. It hosted debates on Southern secession before the Civil War.

  3. 03

    Auburn University Chapel

    College St at Thach Ave, Auburn, AL 36849

    Built in 1851 as a Presbyterian church, the chapel was renovated in a Gothic style around 1900. It is the oldest campus building still on its original site.

    Cater Hall

    277 W Thach Ave, Auburn, AL 36849

    Joseph Hudnut designed this Classical Revival house, built in 1915 as the home of the college president. It later became the social center for the women's Quad dormitories.

2 traditions to check off

Stamped! keeps these as a checklist, so you can tick each one off on the visit.

  • Toomer's Corner

    Where College Street meets Magnolia Avenue, fans celebrate Auburn wins by rolling the oaks with toilet paper, a celebration the university endorses. Visit the corner and the replanted Toomer's Oaks, or come after a win to watch.

  • Tiger Walk

    Before home football games, fans line Donahue Drive to cheer the team as it walks into Jordan-Hare Stadium. Auburn calls it one of the most copied traditions in college football.
