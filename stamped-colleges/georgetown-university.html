Planning the visit

  • Fly intoRonald Reagan Washington National Airport (DCA)

    Reagan National is about 15 minutes' drive from campus across the Potomac. Dulles International is farther out, roughly 45 minutes away.

  • Getting aroundWalking / GUTS shuttle

    Georgetown has no Metro station of its own, so the university's free GUTS shuttle buses link campus with nearby Metro stops. The hilltop campus itself is easy to walk.

  • Colors and teams

    Students call Lauinger Library 'Lau'.

    Healy Hall

    Georgetown University, Washington, DC 20057

    Georgetown's flagship building, built in 1877–1879 by the architects of the Library of Congress's Jefferson Building. Inside are the elaborate Gaston Hall and Riggs Library, one of the few cast-iron libraries left in the country.

    Georgetown University Astronomical Observatory

    Georgetown University, Washington, DC 20057

    Father James Curley planned this Greek Revival observatory and supervised its construction, finished in 1844. In 1846 it was used to fix the latitude and longitude of Washington, D.C.

  4. 04

    Lauinger Library

    37th & O Streets NW, Washington, DC 20057

    John Carl Warnecke's Brutalist main library opened in 1970, designed as a modern interpretation of neighbouring Healy Hall, with a spire echoing Healy's clock tower. It holds 1.7 million volumes on six floors.

2 traditions to check off

Stamped! keeps these as a checklist, so you can tick each one off on the visit.

  • Healy Hall Seal

    A mosaic Georgetown seal is set into the ground at the central entrance to Healy Hall, and students walk around it because legend says stepping on it means you won't graduate. Visitors can see the seal and watch students sidestep it.

  • The Exorcist Steps

    The steep stone staircase from Prospect Street down to M Street appears in the final scene of The Exorcist, written by alumnus William Peter Blatty, and Hoyas make a point of running up them. Visitors can climb the public steps and take a photo.

History

Founded 1789

From Wikipedia

Georgetown University is a private Jesuit research university in Washington, D.C., United States. Founded by Bishop John Carroll in 1789, it is the oldest Catholic institution of higher education in the United States, the oldest university in Washington, D.C., and the nation's first federally chartered university.
