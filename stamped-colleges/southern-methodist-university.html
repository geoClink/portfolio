Southern Methodist University

SMU's first president chose red brick Georgian architecture after Thomas Jefferson's University of Virginia, and Dallas Hall's dome was inspired by the Pantheon.

Planning the visit

  • Fly intoDallas Love Field (DAL)

    Dallas Love Field, a few miles west of campus, is the closest airport. DFW International is farther out.

  • Getting aroundWalking / SMU Express shuttles / DART

    The campus is walkable. Free SMU Express shuttles link campus with DART's SMU/Mockingbird Station on the Red, Blue and Orange lines.

  • Colors and teams

    The school colors are red and blue, and the teams are the Mustangs.

2 traditions to check off

Stamped! keeps these as a checklist, so you can tick each one off on the visit.

  • SMU Seal in Dallas Hall

    The SMU seal is set into the rotunda floor of Dallas Hall. First-year students walk around it at the Rotunda Passage and across it at graduation; visitors can see it in the rotunda.

  • Celebration of Lights

    At the holiday Celebration of Lights, Dallas Hall is covered in thousands of lights. Guests gather on the lawn for cocoa and cookies, and the evening ends with Silent Night.
