Planning the visit

  • Fly intoLubbock Preston Smith International Airport (LBB)

    Lubbock Preston Smith International is the main airport for the Lubbock area.

  • Getting aroundWalking / Citibus

    Citibus runs free loops around campus, such as the Red Raider and Double T routes, and Texas Tech students ride off-campus Citibus routes free with their ID.

  • Colors and teams

    The school colors are scarlet and black, and the teams are the Red Raiders.

  3. 03

    Dairy Barn

    Texas Tech University campus, Lubbock, TX

    Built in 1926 and 1927 to teach dairy science, the barn was used for 40 years. Students raised money in the early 1990s to save it as a symbol of the college's farming roots.

    Agricultural Pavilion

    Texas Tech University campus, Lubbock, TX

    One of the original campus buildings, finished in 1925 as the Livestock Judging Pavilion in the Mission Revival style. Texas Tech basketball played here until the gymnasium opened in 1927.

3 traditions to check off

Stamped! keeps these as a checklist, so you can tick each one off on the visit.

  • Victory Bells

    The Victory Bells in the east tower of the Administration Building ring after every Texas Tech athletic win and at graduations. Listen for them after a win.

  • Carol of Lights

    Since 1959, Texas Tech has covered the Administration Building and nearby buildings in holiday lights each December. Come see the lights at the start of the season.

  • Masked Rider Statue

    A larger-than-life sculpture by Grant Speed honoring the Masked Rider mascot stands outside the Frazier Alumni Pavilion. Take a photo with it.

History

Founded 1923

From Wikipedia

Texas Tech University is a public research university in Lubbock, Texas, United States. Established in 1923 and called Texas Technological College until 1969, it is the flagship institution of the five-institution Texas Tech University System. As of fall 2025, the university enrolled 42,455 students, making it the sixth-largest university in Texas.
