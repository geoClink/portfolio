Planning the visit

  • Fly intoState College Regional Airport (SCE)

    State College Regional Airport, called University Park Airport until 2023, is the nearest airport.

  • Getting aroundWalking / CATA buses

    CATA runs the fare-free Blue Loop, White Loop and Red Link on campus, and community routes to the rest of State College.

  • Colors and teams

    The school colors are blue and white, and the teams are the Nittany Lions.

    Rec Hall

    Curtin Rd, University Park, PA 16802

    650 Bigler Rd, University Park, PA 16802

  4. 04

    Eisenhower Auditorium

    Penn State University Park, PA 16802

    Built from 1971 to 1973 to a plan by consultants Sanders & Bicksler and Bolt Beranek and Newman, this Brutalist-inspired hall opened in 1974. It was first named University Auditorium.

2 traditions to check off

Stamped! keeps these as a checklist, so you can tick each one off on the visit.

  • Nittany Lion Shrine

    Heinz Warneke's limestone lion, dedicated in 1942, is the most photographed spot on campus. Many students take a photo here on their first day and again before graduation.

  • Berkey Creamery

    The university's creamery, whose ice cream short course dates back to 1892, sells its own ice cream in the Food Science Building. Stop in for a cone.
