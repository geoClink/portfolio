Ireland's Oldest University

Trinity College Dublin

Trinity's Old Library holds the 8th-century Book of Kells, and its barrel-vaulted Long Room is one of the most famous library interiors in the world.

Planning the visit

  • Fly intoDublin Airport (DUB)

    Dublin Airport is north of the city, and express buses run from the terminals to the city centre, stopping close to Trinity's front gate.

  • Getting aroundWalking / Luas / Bus

    Trinity sits right in the centre of Dublin, so the campus is easy to reach on foot. Luas trams and many city bus routes stop around its edges.

  • Colors and teams

    English is the everyday language on campus, while Irish is Ireland's first official language and appears on signs; Trinity's Irish name is Coláiste na Tríonóide.

    College Street, Dublin 2

    Museum Building

    New Square, Trinity College, Dublin 2

    Deane and Woodward's palazzo, inspired by the Byzantine buildings of Venice, is covered in over 108 carved capitals. Its domed central hall makes heavy use of Irish stone, with Romanesque arches of alternating yellow and red blocks.

    The Printing House

    Trinity College, Dublin 2

    Richard Castle's little temple, with a pedimented portico of Portland stone, was built around 1734 and is one of Dublin's earliest Palladian buildings. It housed the Dublin University Press until 1976.

  5. 05

    Provost's House

    1 Grafton Street, Dublin 2

    Built in 1759 for Provost Francis Andrews, this Palladian house with its Venetian window faces Grafton Street. It is the only one of Dublin's great Georgian houses still serving its original purpose.

2 traditions to check off

Stamped! keeps these as a checklist, so you can tick each one off on the visit.

  • Don't Walk Under the Campanile

    Student superstition says that anyone who walks under the Campanile in Front Square while its bell tolls will fail their exams, so students rarely shelter beneath it, even in rain. Visitors can watch students walk around it rather than through it.

  • See the Book of Kells and the Long Room

    The Old Library has held the Book of Kells since 1661, and its Long Room gets over 900,000 visitors a year. Visitors can buy a ticket to see the manuscript and walk through the Long Room.

History

Founded 1592

From Wikipedia

The College of the Holy and Undivided Trinity of Queen Elizabeth near Dublin, branded by the board as Trinity College, the University of Dublin, and officially incorporated as Trinity College Dublin, is the sole constituent college of the University of Dublin in Ireland. Founded in 1592 by Queen Elizabeth I through a royal charter, it is one of the seven ancient universities of Great Britain and Ireland. As Ireland's oldest university, Trinity contributed to Irish literature during the Victorian and Georgian eras and played a notable role in the recognition of Dublin as a UNESCO City of Literature.
