University of Houston

The Roy G. Cullen Building, finished in 1939, is the oldest building on campus and is believed to be the first building on a U.S. college campus with air conditioning.

Planning the visit

  • Fly intoWilliam P. Hobby Airport (HOU)

    Hobby Airport, southeast of campus, is the closest airport. George Bush Intercontinental (IAH), north of the city, handles most international flights.

  • Getting aroundWalking / METRORail Purple Line

    METRORail's Purple Line has stops along the campus and runs to downtown Houston.

  • Colors and teams

    The school colors are scarlet red and white, and the teams are the Cougars.

  1. 01

    Ezekiel W. Cullen Building

    Cullen Family Plaza, University of Houston, Houston, TX

    Texas architect Alfred C. Finn designed the university's administrative headquarters in the Art Deco style; it opened in 1950. Its east half holds the 1,544-seat Cullen Performance Hall.

    Roy G. Cullen Building

    University of Houston campus, Houston, TX

    University of Houston campus, Houston, TX

2 traditions to check off

Stamped! keeps these as a checklist, so you can tick each one off on the visit.

  • Rub the Paw

    Before big games, fans rub the paws of the two bronze cougar statues in Cullen Family Plaza in front of the E. Cullen Building for luck. Some students rub them before final exams too.

  • Gate 2 Cougar

    On game days, fans also rub the paw of the cougar statue at Gate 2 of the football stadium. Give it a rub for luck.

History

Founded 1927

From Wikipedia

In 1934, HJC was restructured as a four-year degree-granting institution and renamed University of Houston. In 1977, it became the founding member of the University of Houston System. Today, Houston is the third-largest university in Texas, awarding 11,773 degrees for the 2024–2025 academic year. As of 2024, it has a worldwide alumni base of 331,672.
