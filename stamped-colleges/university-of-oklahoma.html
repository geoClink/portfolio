University of Oklahoma

Frank Lloyd Wright coined the term Cherokee Gothic for OU's brick and pale-stone take on Collegiate Gothic, and newer buildings are designed to match it.

Planning the visit

  • Fly intoWill Rogers International Airport (OKC)

    Will Rogers International Airport in Oklahoma City, north of Norman, is the nearest major airport.

  • Getting aroundWalking / CART buses

    The central campus around the South Oval is walkable. OU's CART buses serve campus and nearby apartments, and EMBARK Norman runs the city routes.

  • Colors and teams

    The school colors are crimson and cream, and the teams are the Sooners.

    Boyd House

    407 W Boyd St, Norman, OK 73069

    OU's first president, David Ross Boyd, built this house in 1906. A later president remodeled it between 1915 and 1922 in the Neoclassical Revival style, adding its four Ionic columns.

    Elm Ave, Norman, OK 73019

2 traditions to check off

Stamped! keeps these as a checklist, so you can tick each one off on the visit.

  • The Seed Sower

    A 12-foot bronze Seed Sower by Paul Moore stands by the South Oval, based on the figure in the university seal and a parable told by first president David Ross Boyd. It is dressed up when the team learns its bowl destination.

  • Heisman Park

    On the east side of the football stadium, bronze statues honor Oklahoma's Heisman Trophy winners. It is a popular spot for photos.

History

Founded 1890

From Wikipedia

In Fall 2024, the university had 34,523 students enrolled, most at its main campus in Norman. Employing nearly 4,000 faculty members, the university offers 174 baccalaureate programs, 199 master's programs, 101 doctoral programs, and 88 certificate programs.
