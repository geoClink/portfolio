Planning the visit

  • Fly intoBaton Rouge Metropolitan Airport (BTR)

    Baton Rouge Metropolitan Airport, north of downtown, serves the city.

  • Getting aroundWalking / Tiger Trails buses

    The central campus around the Quad is walkable. LSU's free Tiger Trails buses connect campus with nearby apartments.

  • Colors and teams

    The school colors are purple and gold, and the teams are the Tigers.

    Field House Dr, Louisiana State University, Baton Rouge, LA 70803

    Tiger Stadium

    W Stadium Rd, Baton Rouge, LA 70803

    Opened in 1924 with 12,000 seats, the stadium now holds more than 100,000. In the 1930s dormitories for students were built into its stands.

    French House

    French House, Louisiana State University, Baton Rouge, LA 70803

2 traditions to check off

Stamped! keeps these as a checklist, so you can tick each one off on the visit.

  • Mike the Tiger's habitat

    LSU has kept a live tiger on campus since 1936. Visit Mike in his habitat between Tiger Stadium and the Pete Maravich Assembly Center, which is open to visitors year-round.

  • Victory Hill march

    Before home games, fans line Victory Hill to watch the team, coaches and the Golden Band march from the Greek Theatre toward Tiger Stadium.

History

Founded 1860

From Wikipedia

Louisiana State University and Agricultural and Mechanical College, commonly referred to as Louisiana State University (LSU), is an American public land-grant research university in Baton Rouge, Louisiana, United States. The university was founded in 1860 near Pineville, Louisiana, under the name Louisiana State Seminary of Learning & Military Academy. The current LSU main campus was dedicated in 1926 and consists of more than 250 buildings constructed in the style of Italian Renaissance architect Andrea Palladio, occupying a 650-acre (260 ha) plateau on the banks of the Mississippi River.
