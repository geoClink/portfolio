Planning the visit

  • Fly intoNashville International Airport (BNA)

    Nashville International Airport is southeast of downtown Nashville.

  • Getting aroundWalking / VandyRide

    The campus is walkable. VandyRide shuttles run around campus, and WeGo city buses run along West End Avenue.

  • Colors and teams

    The school colors are black and gold, and the teams are the Commodores.

    Kirkland Hall

    Kirkland Hall, Vanderbilt University, Nashville, TN 37235

    Designed by William Crawford Smith and built in 1874 as Vanderbilt's first teaching building, the hall burned in 1905. It was rebuilt in 1906 in the Italianate style with a single 52-meter tower.

  2. 02

    Alumni Hall

    2205 West End Ave, Nashville, TN 37235

    Henry C. Hibbs designed this Collegiate Gothic hall, built in 1925 to honor the 44 Vanderbilt men who died in World War I. It was renovated in 2013 and serves as a student center.

  3. 03

    Old Gym

    2301 West End Ave, Nashville, TN 37235

    Completed in 1880 with an indoor running track, this Victorian building is one of the few survivors of the early campus. It later held the fine arts department and now houses admissions.

  4. 04

    Home Economics Building

    134 Magnolia Cir, Nashville, TN 37203

    Ludlow and Peabody designed this red brick building, one of the first two on the new Peabody College campus in 1912. Its columns and swags follow the classical model of the University of Virginia.

    Mayborn Building

    130 Magnolia Cir, Nashville, TN 37203

    Completed in 1914 as the Manual Training building, this is the oldest building on the Peabody campus. Ludlow and Peabody designed it with limestone facings and a well-known smokestack.

1 tradition to check off

Stamped! keeps these as a checklist, so you can tick each one off on the visit.

  • Commodore Vanderbilt statue

    The statue of Cornelius Vanderbilt, whose gift founded the university, stands at the north end of the esplanade facing West End Avenue. The statue is a site for Founder's Day ceremonies around March 17, the anniversary of the founding.
