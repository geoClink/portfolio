Home of the Lakers

Grand Valley State University

Grand Valley is one of only three institutions in the Western Hemisphere with more than one carillon tower: the Cook Carillon in Allendale and the Beckering Family Carillon in Grand Rapids.

Planning the visit

  • Fly intoGerald R. Ford International Airport (GRR)

    Gerald R. Ford International Airport, southeast of Grand Rapids, is the nearest airport with airline service.

  • Getting aroundWalking / Laker Line bus

    The Laker Line bus, run by The Rapid, links the Allendale campus with GVSU's campuses in downtown Grand Rapids along Lake Michigan Drive, and it is open to the public.

  • Colors and teams

    The school colors are blue, black and white, and the teams are the Lakers.

    This 10-story brick and stone clock tower, finished in 1994, is the focal point of the Allendale campus. Its 48 bronze bells were cast by the Royal Eijsbouts foundry in the Netherlands.

    Kirkhof Center

    South Campus Dr, Allendale, MI 49401

    Dedicated in 1974 as the Campus Center, the student union was renamed for Russel H. Kirkhof in 1984. It holds dining, meeting rooms and student offices, and the Laker Line bus stops outside.

  3. 03

    Mary Idema Pew Library

    10582 S Campus Dr, Allendale, MI 49401

    SHW Group designed this library, which opened in June 2013 as the intellectual heart of the Allendale campus. It was the first library in Michigan to earn LEED Platinum certification.

  4. 04

    Lubbers Stadium

    4415 Stadium Dr, Allendale, MI 49401

    Opened in 1973 as Lakers Stadium and later named for president Arend Lubbers, this is the home of Grand Valley football. It seats 10,444 and drew a record 17,007 fans in 2021.

    GVSU Fieldhouse Arena

    Grand Valley State University, Allendale, MI 49401

    Opened in 1982 after the roof of the old "Dome" was condemned, this 4,200-seat arena is home to Lakers basketball and volleyball. It also hosts commencement and concerts.

5 traditions to check off

Stamped! keeps these as a checklist, so you can tick each one off on the visit.

  • Little Mac Bridge and the Ravines

    Students voted the ravines and the Little Mac Bridge the most beautiful spot on campus. Cross the footbridge and look down at the stream below.

  • Carillon Music at Cook Tower

    Every week the university carillonist plays music, from Bach to pop songs, on the bells of the Cook Carillon Tower. If you hear bells, someone is up there playing live.

  • Zumberge Pond

    Lakers love to study, eat or just relax on the steps beside Zumberge Pond in any season. Take a seat and enjoy the view.

  • View from the Library Roof

    The roof of the Mary Idema Pew Library gives a sweeping view of the Allendale campus, a student favorite when the leaves change in fall. Go up and take in the view.

  • Laker Football at Lubbers Stadium

    Lubbers Stadium officially seats 10,444 but often holds thousands more fans, with a record crowd of 17,007 in 2021. Join them for a Lakers home game.
