Planning the visit

  • Fly intoMorgantown Municipal Airport (MGW)

    Morgantown Municipal Airport has United Express flights to Washington Dulles and Chicago O'Hare. Pittsburgh International (PIT) is the nearest large airport.

  • Getting aroundWalking / Personal Rapid Transit (PRT)

    WVU's Personal Rapid Transit runs small driverless cars on a guideway linking downtown Morgantown, the Evansdale campus and the Health Sciences campus.

  • Colors and teams

    The school colors are old gold and blue, and the teams are the Mountaineers.

5 buildings to stamp

  1. 01

    Woodburn Circle

    University Ave, Morgantown, WV

    Three halls built between 1870 and 1893 around an oval path: Martin, Woodburn and Chitwood. Woodburn Hall, with its mansard roof and Italianate tower, is the symbol of the university.

    Built
    1876
    Architect
    C. C. Kemble; Elmer F. Jacobs
    Style

    Today

  2. 02

    Stewart Hall

    West Virginia University campus, Morgantown, WV

    Built from 1900 to 1902 of sandstone and concrete, with a tall octagonal tower and a two-story arcaded rotunda inside. It was the library until 1931 and is now the administration building.

    Built
    1902
    Architect
    Wilson Bros. & Co.
    Style
    Romanesque Revival
    Today
    Administration Building
  3. 03

    Elizabeth Moore Hall

    University Ave, Morgantown, WV

    Built from 1926 to 1928 for women's physical education, this red brick Georgian Revival building has five front arches on Doric columns. It now holds the dean of students and the dance program.

    Built
    1928
    Architect
    Davis, Dunlap & Barney
    Style

  4. 04

    Oglebay Hall

    University Ave, Morgantown, WV

    A 1918 Classical Revival classroom building with four Doric columns under a pediment. It housed the College of Agriculture until 1961, a reminder of WVU's land-grant roots.

    Built
    1918
    Style

  5. 05

    Purinton House

    University Ave, Morgantown, WV

    Built in 1904, this house with a wide Ionic porch was the home of university presidents from 1905 to 1967. President William Howard Taft gave a speech from its porch in 1911.

    Built
    1904
    Architect

2 traditions to check off

Stamped! keeps these as a checklist, so you can tick each one off on the visit.

  • Woodburn Hall Holiday Lighting

    WVU lights up Woodburn Hall for the holidays, a campus tradition revived in 2025. Visit in December to see it lit.

  • Mountaineer Statue

    Sculptor Donald De Lue's 1971 bronze statue of the Mountaineer stands at the Mountainlair student union. Find it and take a photo.
