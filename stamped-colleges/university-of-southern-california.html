Planning the visit

  • Fly intoLos Angeles International Airport (LAX)

    LAX, southwest of campus, is the main airport for Los Angeles.

  • Getting aroundWalking / Metro E Line

    The Metro E Line has stations next to campus along Exposition Park, and USC runs its own campus shuttles.

  • Colors and teams

    The school colors are cardinal and gold, and the teams are the Trojans.

    Widney Alumni House

    650 Childs Way, Los Angeles, CA 90089

    Leavey Library

    University Park Campus, Los Angeles, CA 90089

    Completed in 1994, this undergraduate library returned to the Romanesque Revival look of the older campus buildings. It is named for Thomas E. Leavey, founder of Farmers Insurance.

    Wallis Annenberg Hall

    University Park Campus, Los Angeles, CA 90089

    Harley Ellis Devereaux designed this $150 million building for the Annenberg School, built of concrete and steel with brick veneer in the Collegiate Gothic style. It opened in 2014.

2 traditions to check off

Stamped! keeps these as a checklist, so you can tick each one off on the visit.

  • Tommy Trojan

    The bronze Trojan warrior in Hahn Plaza is the campus's favorite meeting place. Before the UCLA game it is wrapped in duct tape and guarded around the clock by the Trojan Knights.

  • Hecuba Statue

    The statue of Hecuba, queen of Troy, was unveiled in 2017 in the central plaza of USC Village as a counterpart to Tommy Trojan. Visit her in the plaza.

History

Founded 1880

From Wikipedia

The University of Southern California is a private research university in Los Angeles, California, United States. Founded in 1880 by Robert M. Widney, it is the oldest private research university in California and has an enrollment of more than 47,000 students.
