Cornell University

McGraw Tower, built as part of Uris Library in 1891, houses the Cornell Chimes, the first chimes housed and rung on an American college campus. They still play three times a day during term.

Planning the visit

  • Fly intoIthaca Tompkins International Airport (ITH)

    Ithaca's small airport is a few miles north of campus. Many visitors instead fly into Syracuse and drive about an hour south.

  • Getting aroundWalking / TCAT buses

    Cornell's hilltop campus is spread out and steep in places, so comfortable shoes help. TCAT buses connect campus with downtown Ithaca.

  • Colors and teams

    Students say 'Libe Slope' for the hillside that Uris Library overlooks.

  1. 01

    Uris Library

    160 Ho Plaza, Ithaca, NY 14853

    Cornell's oldest library, opened in 1891 with McGraw Tower and its famous chimes rising beside it. Inside is the A. D. White Library, a 'library within a library' named for the university's first president.

    Morrill Hall

    159 Central Ave, Ithaca, NY 14853

    Completed in 1866 and named for Senator Justin Morrill, author of the land-grant act that helped found the university, this hall was declared a National Historic Landmark in 1965.

    1866
    Architect
    Harlow Wilcox and Cyrus K. Porter
    Style

  3. 03

    A. D. White House

    Cornell University, Ithaca, NY 14853

    The richly carved stone mansion that Cornell's first president commissioned in 1871 as a home and a source of artistic inspiration for students. It was later the university art museum and now houses the Society for the Humanities.

    Built
    1871
    Architect
    William Henry Miller and Charles Babcock
    Style
    High Victorian Gothic
    Today
    Society for the Humanities
  4. 04

    Willard Straight Hall

    Ho Plaza, Ithaca, NY 14853

    Cornell's student union, built of local bluestone and opened in 1925 as a memorial to Willard Straight. Its Gothic halls were the scene of the 1969 student protest that reshaped the university.

    Built
    1925
    Architect
    Delano & Aldrich
    Style
    Collegiate Gothic
    Today
    Student union
  5. 05

    Bailey Hall

    Cornell University, Ithaca, NY 14853

    Cornell's largest auditorium is a Greek Revival amphitheatre of 1912, wrapped by a colonnaded portico with monumental stairs up to its main doors. Its acoustics were once praised as 'by God', its wooden seats less so.

    Built
    1912
    Architect
    Edward Brodhead Green (Green & Wicks)
    Style
    Greek Revival
    Today
    Auditorium

3 traditions to check off

Stamped! keeps these as a checklist, so you can tick each one off on the visit.

  • Founders' statues and footprints

    Legend says the statues of Ezra Cornell and Andrew Dickson White will walk off their pedestals and shake hands on the Arts Quad under certain impossible conditions, and painted footprints between them mark the legend. Look for the footprints and pose between the two founders.

  • Cornell Chimes in McGraw Tower

    Student chimesmasters play the 21-bell Cornell Chimes in McGraw Tower in three concerts a day during the academic year, and visitors are welcome at all of them. Listen from Ho Plaza or the Arts Quad.

  • Dragon Day

    On the Friday before spring break, first-year architecture students parade a huge handmade dragon past Sibley Hall toward the Arts Quad. Visitors can line the route and watch it go by.
