Planning the visit

  • Fly intoMinneapolis–Saint Paul International Airport (MSP)

    MSP is south of the Twin Cities. From campus, take the Green Line light rail downtown and change to the Blue Line.

  • Getting aroundWalking / Campus Connector / Green Line

    The free Campus Connector bus links the East Bank, West Bank and St. Paul campuses, and the Metro Green Line light rail runs through campus.

  • Colors and teams

    The school colors are maroon and gold, and the teams are the Golden Gophers.

    84 Church St SE, Minneapolis, MN 55455

    Clarence H. Johnston designed this Classical Revival auditorium, built in 1928–29 at the north end of Northrop Mall. It was rebuilt inside by HGA and reopened in 2014.

    Walter Library

    117 Pleasant St SE, Minneapolis, MN 55455

    State Architect Clarence H. Johnston designed this Beaux-Arts library, built between 1922 and 1924 on Northrop Mall. It now houses the science and engineering library.

    Coffman Memorial Union

    300 Washington Ave SE, Minneapolis, MN 55455
