Linnaeus's university

Uppsala University

Carl Linnaeus owned the university's first botanical garden from 1741 and rearranged it to his own plan; it has been restored to look as it did in the 18th century.

Planning the visit

  • Fly intoStockholm Arlanda Airport (ARN)

    Arlanda, Stockholm's main airport, lies between Stockholm and Uppsala. Trains run from the airport north to Uppsala Central Station.

  • Getting aroundWalking / Bike

    The historic university buildings cluster around the cathedral and University Park, all within a short walk of each other. Uppsala is also a famously bike-friendly city.

  • Colors and teams

    Swedish is the local language, and many programmes are taught in English. Student life revolves around the 'nations', student societies named after regions of Sweden.

    University Hall

    University Park, Uppsala

    Uppsala University's main building stands in University Park near the cathedral. Herman Teodor Holmgren designed it in an Italian Renaissance Beaux-Arts style, and it was completed in 1887.

  2. 02

    Gustavianum

    Akademigatan 3, Uppsala

    Built between 1622 and 1625, the Gustavianum is the oldest standing building of Uppsala University and was expanded by Olaus Rudbeck in 1662. It was the main university building from 1778 to 1887 and has been the university museum since 1997.

    Built
    1625
    Architect
    Caspar van Panten; Olaus Rudbeck (1662 expansion)
    Style
    17th-century Renaissance
    Today
    University museum
  3. 03

    Carolina Rediviva

    Carolina Rediviva, Uppsala

    Sweden's oldest and largest university library building was begun in 1820 and completed in 1841. It is where the Codex Argenteus, the 'Silver Bible', is kept.

    Built
    1841
    Architect
    Carl Fredrik Sundvall
    Style
    Neoclassical
    Today

  4. 04

    Linnaean Garden

    Linnéträdgården, Uppsala

    Olaus Rudbeck planted this garden in 1655, and Carl Linnaeus later owned it and rearranged it to his own design. Restored to his specifications, it is kept as an 18th-century botanical garden.

    Built
    1655
    Architect
    Olaus Rudbeck; Carl Linnaeus (redesign)
    Style
    18th-century botanical garden
    Today
    Historic botanical garden

3 traditions to check off

Stamped! keeps these as a checklist, so you can tick each one off on the visit.

  • Walpurgis Cap Raising

    At 3 pm on 30 April, students on the slope below the Carolina Rediviva library put on their white student caps together as the rector waves his cap from the library balcony. Anyone can join the crowd on the hill to watch.

  • Spring Song at Gunillaklockan

    On the evening of 30 April, students gather at the Gunilla bell beside Uppsala Castle for the spring song and the spring speech. Visitors can stand with the crowd and listen.

  • Gustavianum Anatomical Theatre

    Under the copper cupola of the Gustavianum, the university museum preserves the anatomical theatre Olof Rudbeck the Elder built in the 1660s. Visitors can step inside it as part of a museum visit.
