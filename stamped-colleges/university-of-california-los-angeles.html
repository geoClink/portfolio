Home of the Bruins

University of California, Los Angeles

Royce Hall and Powell Library, two of the four original buildings on the Westwood campus, face each other across the main quad. Both are brick Romanesque Revival buildings modelled on the Basilica of Sant'Ambrogio in Milan.

Planning the visit

  • Fly intoLos Angeles International Airport (LAX)

    LAX is the closest major airport to Westwood, usually a short drive north along the San Diego Freeway, depending on traffic.

  • Getting aroundWalking / Metro Bus

    The central campus is compact enough to explore on foot. Many Los Angeles Metro bus lines stop around the edges of the Westwood campus.

  • Colors and teams

    Students are Bruins, and the twin towers of Royce Hall are the image of UCLA you will see on almost every brochure.

4 buildings to stamp

  1. 01

    Royce Hall

    340 Royce Drive, Los Angeles, CA 90095

    The twin-towered brick front of Royce Hall, inspired by Milan's Basilica of Sant'Ambrogio, is the best-known landmark at UCLA. Its 1,800-seat auditorium was built for lectures but grew into a major concert hall.

    Built
    1929
    Architect
    Allison & Allison
    Style
    Romanesque Revival
    Today
    Concert hall and performing arts venue
  2. 02

    Powell Library

    Powell Library, UCLA, Los Angeles, CA 90095

    Facing Royce Hall across the quad, Powell Library is also modelled on Sant'Ambrogio. Its central tower is topped by an octagonal dome, and Renaissance printers' marks decorate the ceiling of the main reading room.

    Built
    1929
    Architect
    George W. Kelham
    Style
    Romanesque Revival
    Today
    Undergraduate library
  3. 03

    Charles E. Young Research Library

    Charles E. Young Research Library, UCLA, Los Angeles, CA 90095

    A Mid-Century Modern library of concrete frame and dark glass at the north edge of campus, designed by A. Quincy Jones and Frederick Earl Emmons. It is one of the largest libraries at UCLA.

    Built
    1964
    Architect
    A. Quincy Jones and Frederick Earl Emmons
    Style
    Mid-Century Modern
    Today
    Research library
  4. 04

    Franklin D. Murphy Sculpture Garden

    Franklin D. Murphy Sculpture Garden, UCLA, Los Angeles, CA 90095

    Landscape architect Ralph Cornell laid out this five-acre garden in 1967. More than 70 sculptures by artists like Henry Moore, Rodin, Calder and Noguchi stand among its lawns and trees.

3 traditions to check off

Stamped! keeps these as a checklist, so you can tick each one off on the visit.

  • Rub the Bruin Bear's Paw

    Students rub the paw of the Bruin Bear statue in Bruin Plaza for good luck during finals week. Visitors stop for a photo and a paw rub.

  • Skip the Sixth Step

    Legend says anyone who steps on the sixth step of Janss Steps will have bad luck and graduate a quarter late. Many students hop over it on the way up.

  • Inverted Fountain

    New students are "Bruintized" at this 1968 fountain, whose water drops into a well instead of spraying up. Tradition says they then avoid touching the water until graduation, so visitors look but do not touch.
