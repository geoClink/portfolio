Planning the visit

  • Fly intoDane County Regional Airport (MSN)

    Dane County Regional Airport, northeast of downtown Madison, is the city's airport.

  • Getting aroundWalking / Madison Metro

    Madison Metro buses cross campus, and students ride with a university bus pass.

  • Colors and teams

    The school colors are cardinal and white, and the teams are the Badgers.

    Washburn Observatory

    1401 Observatory Dr, Madison, WI 53706

    David R. Jones designed this Italianate observatory, completed in 1881. It was a major research center for about 50 years, and its telescope is still used by students and the public.

3 traditions to check off

Stamped! keeps these as a checklist, so you can tick each one off on the visit.

  • Abe Lincoln on Bascom Hill

    The seated Abraham Lincoln statue in front of Bascom Hall is a highlight of commencement weekend photos. Take your picture beside him at the top of the hill.

  • Terrace Sunburst Chairs

    The sunburst chairs on the Memorial Union Terrace are one of the university's best-known symbols. Sit in one by Lake Mendota.

  • Babcock Hall Ice Cream

    Ice cream made by the university's dairy at Babcock Hall is a student staple and a visitor favorite. Try a scoop.

History

Founded 1848

From Wikipedia

The University of Wisconsin–Madison is a public land-grant research university in Madison, Wisconsin, United States. It was founded in 1848 when Wisconsin achieved statehood and is the flagship campus of the University of Wisconsin System. The 933-acre (378 ha) main campus is located on the shores of Lake Mendota; the university also owns and operates a 1,200-acre (486 ha) arboretum 4 miles (6.4 km) south of the main campus.
