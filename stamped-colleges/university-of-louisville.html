Planning the visit

  • Fly intoLouisville Muhammad Ali International Airport (SDF)

    Louisville Muhammad Ali International is a short drive south of Belknap Campus.

  • Getting aroundWalking / TARC buses

    Belknap Campus is walkable. Students and staff ride TARC buses free with a UofL ID, including Route 28 to the Health Sciences Center.

  • Colors and teams

    The school colors are red and black, and the teams are the Cardinals.

4 buildings to stamp

  1. 01

    Grawemeyer Hall

    2301 S 3rd St, Louisville, KY 40292

    Completed in 1926 and modeled on Jefferson's Rotunda, this domed building houses the president's office. A Foucault pendulum hangs 72 feet under the dome.

    Built
    1926
    Architect
    Allied Architects of Kentucky
    Style
    Neoclassical
    Today
    Administration Building
  2. 02

    University Playhouse

    1911 S 3rd St, Louisville, KY 40208

    Built in 1874 as the chapel of the orphanage and reform school that once stood on the Belknap grounds, this wooden building became the university's theater in 1925. It was taken apart plank by plank in 1977 to make way for Ekstrom Library and reopened in 1980.

    Built
    1874
    Today
    Theater
  3. 03

    Ekstrom Library

    2301 S 3rd St, Louisville, KY 40292

    The university's main library opened in 1981 in a building by the Louisville firm Louis & Henry, which won a Kentucky Society of Architects merit award. It holds about two million historic photographs.

    Built
    1981
    Architect
    Louis & Henry
    Today
    Library
  4. 04

    L&N Federal Credit Union Stadium

    2800 S Floyd St, Louisville, KY 40209

    The football stadium opened in 1998 at the south end of campus in a horseshoe shape designed to expand.

    Built
    1998
    Architect
    Rosser International; Luckett & Farley
    Today
    Football Stadium

1 tradition to check off

Stamped! keeps these as a checklist, so you can tick each one off on the visit.

  • The Thinker

    A large bronze cast of Rodin's The Thinker has sat in front of Grawemeyer Hall since 1949. Having your picture taken with it is a classic stop on a campus visit.
