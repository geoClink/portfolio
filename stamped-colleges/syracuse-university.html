Planning the visit

  • Fly intoSyracuse Hancock International Airport (SYR)

    Syracuse Hancock International, north of the city, is the nearest commercial airport.

  • Getting aroundWalking / Centro buses

    Main Campus is walkable. Centro runs Syracuse University's campus bus routes as well as city buses to downtown Syracuse.

  • Colors and teams

    The school color is orange, and the teams are the Orange.

  1. 01

    Hall of Languages

    Syracuse, NY 13244

    Horatio Nelson White designed the university's first building in the Second Empire style, built in 1871–1873. For its first years it held the entire university.

    1873
    Architect

  2. 02

    Crouse College

    Syracuse, NY 13244

    Archimedes Russell designed this Richardsonian Romanesque hall of brownstone, completed in 1889 as a women's college. Its high roofs, gables and round arches make it a campus landmark.

    1889
    Architect

    Style

  3. 03

    Hendricks Chapel

    Syracuse, NY 13244

    John Russell Pope and Dwight James Baum designed this domed chapel, completed in 1930, drawing on Palladio and the Pantheon. It is the multi-faith center of the university.

    Built
    1930
    Architect

  4. 04

    Carnegie Library

    130 Sims Dr, Syracuse, NY 13210

    Professors Frederick Revels and Earl Hallenbeck designed this Renaissance-style library, opened in 1907 with Andrew Carnegie's gift. Ionic columns frame the front, and the reading room has a high vaulted ceiling.

    Built
    1907
    Architect
    Frederick W. Revels and Earl Hallenbeck
    Style

    Today

  5. 05

    JMA Wireless Dome

    900 Irving Ave, Syracuse, NY 13244

    Opened in 1980 as the Carrier Dome, this domed stadium is the home of Orange football, basketball and lacrosse. A major renovation in 2021–2022 gave it a new roof.

    Built
    1980
    Architect

    Today

2 traditions to check off

Stamped! keeps these as a checklist, so you can tick each one off on the visit.

  • Kissing Bench

    This granite bench between Tolley Hall and the Hall of Languages was a gift from the class of 1912. Legend says a couple who kisses on it will marry, and anyone who sits there alone will stay single.

  • Crouse Chimes

    The chimes in the tower of Crouse College have rung since 1889 and are played twice a day and on special occasions. Visitors listen from the Quad below.

History

Founded 1870

From Wikipedia

Located in the city's University Hill neighborhood, east and southeast of downtown Syracuse, the large campus features an eclectic mix of architecture, ranging from nineteenth-century Romanesque Revival to contemporary buildings. Syracuse University is organized into 13 schools and colleges and in the Carnegie Classification of Institutions of Higher Education is classified as "R1: Doctoral Universities – Very high research activity".
