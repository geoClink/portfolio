Planning the visit

  • Fly intoMcGhee Tyson Airport (TYS)

    McGhee Tyson Airport in Alcoa, south of Knoxville, is the nearest airport.

  • Getting aroundWalking / T buses

    UT's free T buses run fixed routes across campus, and students ride Knoxville Area Transit buses free with their VolCard.

  • Colors and teams

    The school colors are orange and white, and the teams are the Volunteers.

  1. 01

    Ayres Hall

    1403 Circle Dr, Knoxville, TN 37996

    Miller, Fullenwider and Dowling of Chicago designed this Gothic Revival hall on The Hill, completed in 1921. Its clock tower rises 43 meters and is the best-known symbol of the university.

  2. 02

    McClung Museum

    Circle Park, University of Tennessee, Knoxville, TN 37996

    UT architect Malcolm Rice and the firm Barber McMurry designed this modernist museum, the first university building on Circle Park. It holds collections of natural history, archaeology and decorative arts.

  3. 03

    John C. Hodges Library

    1015 Volunteer Blvd, Knoxville, TN 37996

    The 1969 undergraduate library was wrapped by a 1987 expansion that more than tripled its size. The interior uses Italian and Spanish marble and is designed to let in natural light.

    Built
    1969
    Architect
    McCarty Holsaple McCarty; Cooper and Perry; Lindsey and Maples (1987 expansion)
    Today
    Main Library
  4. 04

    Neyland Stadium

    1235 Phillip Fulmer Way, Knoxville, TN 37916

    Opened in 1921 as Shields–Watkins Field, the stadium has grown through 16 expansions to about 102,000 seats. It is named for coach Robert Neyland.

    Built
    1921
    Architect
    Unknown; McCarty Holsaple McCarty (renovations)
    Today

3 traditions to check off

Stamped! keeps these as a checklist, so you can tick each one off on the visit.

  • The Torchbearer

    The Torchbearer statue in Circle Park carries the Volunteer Creed on its plaque. Read the creed, the source of the university's Torch Night ceremony.

  • The Rock

    This large limestone boulder is painted over and over by students with messages, from birthdays to marriage proposals. Stop by to see what it says today.

  • Vol Walk

    Before home games, fans line the route as the team walks from the Torchbearer to Neyland Stadium, led by the band. The tradition dates to 1990.
