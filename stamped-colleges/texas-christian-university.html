Go Frogs

Texas Christian University

Most TCU buildings use a golden brick blend called "TCU buff" with red tile roofs. Robert Carr Chapel broke the rule with salmon-colored brick, and alumni protested when it opened.

Planning the visit

  • Fly intoDallas/Fort Worth International Airport (DFW)

    DFW Airport, northeast of Fort Worth, is the main airport for the region.

  • Getting aroundWalking / Trinity Metro

    The campus is compact and walkable. Trinity Metro runs Fort Worth's public buses and trains.

  • Colors and teams

    The school colors are purple and white, and the teams are the Horned Frogs.

4 buildings to stamp

  1. 01

    Amon G. Carter Stadium

    2850 Stadium Dr, Fort Worth, TX

    TCU's football stadium opened in 1930 and was rebuilt from 2010 to 2012 by HKS in a Southwestern Art Deco style, using TCU brick, arches and stone details.

  2. 02

    Robert Carr Chapel

    Texas Christian University campus, Fort Worth, TX

    Joseph R. Pelich designed TCU's chapel in the Georgian Colonial Revival style, and it opened in May 1954. Its spire copies the one on the Old Lyme church in Connecticut, and its salmon brick sets it apart from the rest of campus.

    Built
    1954
    Architect
    Joseph R. Pelich
    Style
    Georgian Colonial Revival
    Today

  3. 03

    Schollmaier Arena

    2900 Stadium Dr, Fort Worth, TX 76109

    Built in 1961 as Daniel–Meyer Coliseum, the basketball arena was renovated and renamed in 2015. Its new brick facade echoes the Art Deco look of the neighboring stadium.

3 traditions to check off

Stamped! keeps these as a checklist, so you can tick each one off on the visit.

  • Lucky Horned Frog Statue

    Students rub the nose of the welded Horned Frog statue by Seppo Aarnos, installed in 1984 between Sadler and Reed halls, for luck before tests. Give it a rub.

  • Frog Fountain

    Seniors and their families take cap-and-gown photos at Frog Fountain, and fireworks light the sky over it when the Frogs score at home games. Take a photo at the fountain.

  • Carols by Candlelight

    For more than 40 years, Carols by Candlelight has been held each December in Robert Carr Chapel. Visit the chapel during the holiday season.
