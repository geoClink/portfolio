Planning the visit

  • Fly intoSeattle–Tacoma International Airport (SEA)

    Sea-Tac sits south of Seattle. Link light rail runs from the airport straight to University of Washington station, next to Husky Stadium.

  • Getting aroundWalking / Link light rail

    Link light rail stops at University of Washington station by Husky Stadium. From there the central campus, from Red Square to the Quad, is an easy walk.

  • Colors and teams

    The university is 'U-Dub' to almost everyone, and the red-brick Central Plaza is always called Red Square.

  1. 01

    Suzzallo Library

    Suzzallo Library, University of Washington, Seattle, WA 98195

    The Collegiate Gothic façade of Suzzallo dominates Red Square, and its Graduate Reading Room is one of the most photographed rooms in Seattle. The architects planned a 300-foot bell tower that was never built.

    Denny Hall, University of Washington, Seattle, WA 98195

    Liberal Arts Quadrangle, University of Washington, Seattle, WA 98195

    The Liberal Arts Quadrangle is UW's trademark: brick Gothic halls framing a lawn lined with thirty Yoshino cherry trees. Carl Gould drew its layout in 1915, though it was not finished until 1950.

    Henry Art Gallery

    4100 15th Ave NE, Seattle, WA 98105

4 traditions to check off

Stamped! keeps these as a checklist, so you can tick each one off on the visit.

  • Cherry Blossoms in the Quad

    Visiting the Yoshino cherry trees in the Quad when they bloom each spring is a must-do UW tradition. Expect crowds of students and visitors taking photos at peak bloom.

  • Rainier Vista

    The view down Rainier Vista past Drumheller Fountain is protected so nothing can block Mount Rainier from Red Square. On a clear day, stand at the top for the best view on campus.

  • Sylvan Grove Columns

    Four cedar columns from the UW's first downtown building stand in Sylvan Grove and represent loyalty, industry, faith and efficiency. New students touch one when they arrive and again at graduation; visitors can see them in the grove.

  • The Wave at Husky Stadium

    UW says the famous stadium wave began at Husky Stadium during a 1981 football game. Join in when it rolls around the stands on game day.
