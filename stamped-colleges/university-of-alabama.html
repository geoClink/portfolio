Planning the visit

  • Fly intoBirmingham–Shuttlesworth International Airport (BHM)

    Birmingham–Shuttlesworth International Airport, northeast of Tuscaloosa, is the nearest airport with regular airline service.

  • Getting aroundWalking / CrimsonRide buses

    The Quad sits at the heart of a walkable campus. The university's free CrimsonRide buses run campus and apartment routes.

  • Colors and teams

    The school colors are crimson and white, and the teams are the Crimson Tide.

  1. 01

    Denny Chimes

    The Quad, University of Alabama, Tuscaloosa, AL 35487

    This 35-meter Art Deco campanile on the south side of the Quad was dedicated in 1929 and named for university president George H. Denny. Its brick shaft tapers to a limestone belfry that holds a 25-bell carillon.

  2. 02

    President's Mansion

    University Blvd, University of Alabama, Tuscaloosa, AL 35487

    Completed in 1841 to a design by Michael Barry, this Greek Revival mansion has housed every university president since. It narrowly escaped destruction during the Civil War and is one of the oldest buildings on campus.

    Built
    1841
    Architect
    Michael Barry
    Style
    Greek Revival
    Today
    President's Residence
  3. 03

    Maxwell Hall (Old Observatory)

    University of Alabama, Tuscaloosa, AL 35487

    Completed in 1844, this Greek Revival observatory once held an 8-inch Troughton & Simms refractor under a revolving 18-foot dome. Now called Frederick R. Maxwell Hall, it houses an arts research program.

    Built
    1844
    Style
    Greek Revival
    Today
    Arts Research Center
  4. 04

    Smith Hall

    Smith Hall, University of Alabama, Tuscaloosa, AL 35487

    Smith Hall, completed in 1909 and dedicated in 1910, is a Beaux-Arts building with a three-story central block and two-story wings. It houses the Alabama Museum of Natural History, the oldest museum in the state.

2 traditions to check off

Stamped! keeps these as a checklist, so you can tick each one off on the visit.

  • Walk of Fame at Denny Chimes

    Since 1948, Alabama's football captains have pressed their handprints and footprints into the concrete around the base of Denny Chimes. Walk around the tower to find the names, and listen for the Westminster chimes every 15 minutes.

  • Walk of Champions

    Outside Bryant–Denny Stadium, granite slabs list the Crimson Tide's national championships beside statues of championship coaches. On game days fans line the walk to cheer the team as it arrives.
