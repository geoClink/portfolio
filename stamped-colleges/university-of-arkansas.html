Planning the visit

  • Fly intoNorthwest Arkansas National Airport (XNA)

    Northwest Arkansas National Airport, north of Fayetteville, is the main airport for the region.

  • Getting aroundWalking / Razorback Transit

    The hilltop campus is walkable. Razorback Transit runs fare-free buses on campus and around Fayetteville.

  • Colors and teams

    The school colors are cardinal and white, and the teams are the Razorbacks.

2 traditions to check off

Stamped! keeps these as a checklist, so you can tick each one off on the visit.

  • Senior Walk

    The campus sidewalks carry the names of the university's graduates, class by class, going back to 1876. Start near Old Main and look down to read the names.

  • Spoofer's Stone

    This limestone rock near Old Main dates from a time when men and women students were not allowed to mingle, and they left love notes in its cracks. It is now a popular spot for marriage proposals.
