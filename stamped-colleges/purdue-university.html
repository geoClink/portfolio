Planning the visit

  • Fly intoIndianapolis International Airport (IND)

    Indianapolis International, southeast of West Lafayette, is the nearest major airport.

  • Getting aroundWalking / Purdue Campus Transit / CityBus

    Free Purdue Campus Transit loops circle the campus, and CityBus routes connect West Lafayette with Lafayette.

  • Colors and teams

    The school colors are old gold and black, and the teams are the Boilermakers.

4 buildings to stamp

  1. 01

    Purdue Bell Tower

    Purdue University, West Lafayette, IN 47907

    This 160-foot brick tower, a gift of the class of 1948, was completed in 1995 at the heart of campus. It is a modern take on the old Heavilon Hall tower and has a clock on each face.

    Style

  2. 02

    Elliott Hall of Music

    712 3rd St, West Lafayette, IN 47907

    Walter Scholer designed this 6,005-seat theater, completed in 1940, with J. André Fouilhoux, one of the architects of Radio City Music Hall, as consultant. It is one of the largest proscenium theaters in the world.

    Built
    1940
    Architect
    Walter Scholer; J. André Fouilhoux (consulting)
    Today
    Theater
  3. 03

    Purdue Memorial Union

    101 N Grant St, West Lafayette, IN 47907

    Chicago firm Pond and Pond designed this student union, opened in 1924 as a memorial to Purdue students who fought in World War I. It holds restaurants, lounges, a bowling alley and a hotel.

    Built
    1924
    Architect
    Pond and Pond
    Style
    Gothic Revival
    Today
    Student Union and Hotel
  4. 04

    Engineering Fountain

    Purdue University, West Lafayette, IN 47907

    Sculptor Robert Youngman designed this 38-foot water sculpture of 228 tons of concrete, a gift of the class of 1939. It was dedicated in 1989.

2 traditions to check off

Stamped! keeps these as a checklist, so you can tick each one off on the visit.

  • Bell Tower Kiss

    Legend says a couple who kiss under the Bell Tower and then walk past the nearby Stone Lion Fountain will one day marry. Visit the tower at the center of campus.

  • Hello Walk

    The Hello Walk began as the path to University Hall and now covers the walks across Memorial Mall. Smile and say hello to everyone you pass.
