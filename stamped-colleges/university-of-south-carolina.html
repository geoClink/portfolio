University of South Carolina

The Horseshoe's first building, Rutledge, was finished in 1805, and the South Caroliniana Library on the Horseshoe is the oldest free-standing university library in the United States.

Planning the visit

  • Fly intoColumbia Metropolitan Airport (CAE)

    Columbia Metropolitan Airport is southwest of the city center.

  • Getting aroundWalking / Carolina Shuttle

    The Horseshoe and central campus are walkable. The Carolina Shuttle, run by The COMET, is free for students, faculty and staff.

  • Colors and teams

    The school colors are garnet and black, and the teams are the Gamecocks.

  1. 01

    The Horseshoe

    Horseshoe, Sumter St, Columbia, SC 29208

    The Horseshoe is the original campus of South Carolina College, laid out from plans by Robert Mills and others. Its first building, Rutledge, was completed in 1805.

  2. 02

    Longstreet Theatre

    1300 Greene St, Columbia, SC 29208

    Jacob Graves designed this Greek Revival hall, completed in 1855 as a chapel and lecture hall. Poor acoustics kept it from that role, and after serving as a hospital, arsenal and gym, it became a theatre in the round in 1977.

    Currell College

    Currell College, Columbia, SC 29208

    This two-story red brick building, completed in 1919, is the only one William Augustus Edwards designed for the university. It was built for the law school.

  4. 04

    Horry-Guignard House

    Pendleton St, Columbia, SC 29208

    Built before 1813, this two-story late Federal style frame house was probably built by Revolutionary War colonel Peter Horry. It is now part of the School of Law complex.

2 traditions to check off

Stamped! keeps these as a checklist, so you can tick each one off on the visit.

  • Maxcy Monument

    At the center of the Horseshoe stands the monument to Jonathan Maxcy, the first president, designed by Robert Mills and dedicated in 1827. Read the Latin inscriptions and the English translations nearby.

  • Cocky statue

    A statue of the Gamecock mascot stands off Greene Street near the Russell House, and visitors are invited to take a photo with it.

History

Founded 1801

From Wikipedia

Its main campus is on over 359 acres (145 ha) in downtown Columbia, close to the South Carolina State House. The university is classified among "R1: Doctoral Universities with Highest Research Activity". It houses the largest collection of Robert Burns and Scottish literature materials outside Scotland and the world's largest Ernest Hemingway collection.
