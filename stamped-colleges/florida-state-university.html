Florida State University

Planning the visit

  • Fly intoTallahassee International Airport (TLH)

    Tallahassee International, southwest of the city, is the nearest commercial airport.

  • Getting aroundWalking / Seminole Express

    The campus is walkable. The Seminole Express buses, run with StarMetro, serve campus and nearby neighborhoods, and students ride free with their FSUCard.

  • Colors and teams

    The school colors are garnet and gold, and the teams are the Seminoles.

    Westcott Building

    Florida State University, Tallahassee, FL 32306

    Built in 1910 as the administration building of the Florida State College, this Collegiate Gothic hall closes the view up College Avenue. It holds the university's main offices and Ruby Diamond Auditorium.

  2. 02

    Dodd Hall

    641 University Way, Tallahassee, FL 32306

    Built in 1923 as the library of the Florida State College for Women, Dodd Hall is one of the most ornate Collegiate Gothic buildings on campus. It was the main library until Strozier Library opened in 1956.

    Built
    1923
    Architect
    William Augustus Edwards
    Style
    Collegiate Gothic
    Today

    Legacy Hall

    402 W Gaines St, Tallahassee, FL 32306

    Goody Clancy designed this five-story home for the College of Business, completed in 2025 as the largest academic building on campus. It follows the Collegiate Gothic style of the older campus.

    Student Union

    75 N Woodward Ave, Tallahassee, FL 32306

    The four-story student union opened in 2022 on the site of six older buildings. It is a modern building wrapped in a Collegiate Gothic brick exterior to match the campus.

  5. 05

    Doak Campbell Stadium

    403 Stadium Dr W, Tallahassee, FL 32304

    The football stadium opened in 1950 and later grew into a mixed-use complex with offices, classrooms and the University Center. Its brick facade matches the Collegiate Gothic design of most campus buildings.

2 traditions to check off

Stamped! keeps these as a checklist, so you can tick each one off on the visit.

  • Unconquered Statue

    This bronze statue outside Doak Campbell Stadium shows a Seminole rider on a rearing horse. Its spear is lit at sunset before home football games, and fans gather to watch.

  • Legacy Walk

    Medallions and garnet banners mark a walking tour of campus history that starts at Landis Green. Visitors follow it past historic buildings, statues and monuments.

History

Founded 1851

From Wikipedia

Florida State University is a public research university in Tallahassee, Florida, United States. It is a senior member of the State University System of Florida and a preeminent university in the state. Chartered in 1851, it is located on Florida's oldest continuous site of higher education.
