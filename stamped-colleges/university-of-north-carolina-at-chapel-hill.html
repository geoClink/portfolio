Planning the visit

  • Fly intoRaleigh-Durham International Airport (RDU)

    Raleigh-Durham International, east of Chapel Hill, is the main airport for the Research Triangle.

  • Getting aroundWalking / Chapel Hill Transit

    The campus is walkable. Chapel Hill Transit runs fare-free buses across campus and through Chapel Hill and Carrboro.

  • Colors and teams

    The school colors are Carolina blue and white, and the teams are the Tar Heels.

    Old Well

    Cameron Ave, Chapel Hill, NC 27514

    University registrar Eugene Lewis Harris gave the campus well this neoclassical domed rotunda in 1897, modeled on the Temple of Love at Versailles. It is the best-known symbol of the university.

    Old East

    Cameron Ave, Chapel Hill, NC 27514

    Built in 1793 by enslaved labor, Old East is the first building of any state university in the country. A third story was added in 1823, and it is still a residence hall.

    Playmakers Theatre

    Cameron Ave, Chapel Hill, NC 27514

    Wilson Library

    Chapel Hill, NC 27514

    Arthur Cleveland Nash and William Kendall of McKim, Mead & White designed this neoclassical library, completed in 1929. Its dome rises 85 feet over the south end of the South Quadrangle.

    Morehead-Patterson Bell Tower

    Chapel Hill, NC 27514

    McKim, Mead & White designed this 172-foot brick tower, completed in 1931, with a clock on each side and a Guastavino tile arcade at its base. It is lit Carolina blue after football wins.

3 traditions to check off

Stamped! keeps these as a checklist, so you can tick each one off on the visit.

  • Sip from the Old Well

    Campus tradition says a drink from the fountain at the Old Well on the first day of classes brings good luck. Visitors can take a sip from the fountain any day.

  • Davie Poplar

    Legend says the university will thrive as long as this centuries-old tulip poplar in McCorkle Place stands. Visitors stop to see the tree.

  • Bell Tower in Carolina Blue

    The Morehead-Patterson Bell Tower is lit Carolina blue after Tar Heel football wins. Visitors can see the 172-foot tower on the south side of campus.
