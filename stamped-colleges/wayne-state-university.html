Wayne State University

Old Main, the clock-towered heart of campus, was built in the 1890s as Detroit's Central High School. College classes that started there grew into Wayne State University.

Planning the visit

  • Fly intoDetroit Metropolitan Wayne County Airport (DTW)

    Detroit Metropolitan Wayne County Airport in Romulus, southwest of Detroit, is Michigan's busiest airport and the main gateway to the city.

  • Getting aroundWalking / QLINE streetcar

    The main campus in Midtown is easy to walk. The QLINE streetcar runs along Woodward Avenue at the campus's east edge and links it with Downtown Detroit and New Center.

  • Colors and teams

    The school colors are green and gold, and the teams are the Warriors.

4 traditions to check off

Stamped! keeps these as a checklist, so you can tick each one off on the visit.

  • Friday Night Planetarium Show

    The Wayne State Planetarium in Old Main puts on free public shows most Friday evenings, with a tour of the current night sky and a fulldome film. Arrive early, since seats are first come, first served.

  • Old Main's Peregrine Falcons

    Peregrine falcons live on a balcony of Old Main's clock tower, and the university calls them its winged Warriors. Look up at the tower to spot them.

  • Campus ArtWalk

    Around 30 donated outdoor sculptures make the campus an open-air gallery you can visit any time. Start at "City with Halo" in front of the Thompson Home and follow the ArtWalk map.

  • Homecoming Game at Tom Adams Field

    Homecoming week ends with Warrior football at Tom Adams Field, and fans are asked to wear green and gold. Join the crowd and cheer on the Warriors.

History

Founded 1868

From Wikipedia

Wayne State University (WSU) is a public research university in Detroit, Michigan, United States. Founded in 1868, Wayne State consists of 13 schools and colleges offering approximately 375 programs. It is Michigan's third-largest university with nearly 25,000 graduate and undergraduate students.
