Planning the visit

  • Fly intoManhattan Regional Airport (MHK)

    Manhattan Regional Airport has American Eagle flights to Dallas/Fort Worth and Chicago O'Hare.

  • Getting aroundWalking / aTa Bus

    The campus is walkable. Through a partnership with the aTa Bus, K-State students, faculty and staff ride campus and Manhattan routes free with their K-State ID.

  • Colors and teams

    The school color is royal purple, and the teams are the Wildcats.

4 buildings to stamp

  1. 01

    Anderson Hall

    Kansas State University campus, Manhattan, KS

    Erasmus T. Carr designed this Gothic stone building, whose first wing was finished in 1879. It is the university's central administration building.

    Built
    1879
    Architect
    Erasmus T. Carr
    Style
    Gothic
    Today
    Administration Building
  2. 02

    Nichols Hall

    Kansas State University campus, Manhattan, KS

    Built in 1911, it looks from outside like a castle with battlements. Fire destroyed its interior in 1968, and it was rebuilt in 1985 for journalism and the performing arts.

    Built
    1911
    Style
    Neo-Renaissance
    Stories
    3
    Today
    Media, Music, Theatre and Dance
  3. 03

    Seaton Hall

    Kansas State University campus, Manhattan, KS

    A sprawling building just north of the Student Union, begun with its south wing in 1908 and extended in stages until 1959. It is named for a longtime dean of engineering and architecture.

    Built
    1908
    Style
    Neo-Renaissance
    Stories
    3
    Today
    Architecture and Engineering
  4. 04

    Hale Library

    Kansas State University campus, Manhattan, KS

    The main library grew from a 1927 Romanesque Revival building by Paul Weigel and Charles Cuthbert. Hammond Beeby Babka's 1997 expansion joined the old and new parts and won an AIA regional merit award.

    Built
    1927
    Architect
    Paul Weigel and Charles Cuthbert; Hammond Beeby Babka (1997)
    Style
    Romanesque Revival
    Stories
    5
    Today
