Tulane University

Tulane's Uptown campus faces Audubon Park across St. Charles Avenue. Gibson Hall, built in 1894, was the first academic building on the campus, and the Newcomb College buildings were designed by Yale architect James Gamble Rogers.

Planning the visit

  • Fly intoLouis Armstrong New Orleans International Airport (MSY)

    Louis Armstrong New Orleans International is in Kenner, about 11 miles (18 km) west of downtown New Orleans.

  • Getting aroundWalking / St. Charles Streetcar

    The St. Charles Streetcar stops right in front of the campus and runs to downtown New Orleans. McAlister Place, once a road through the middle of campus, became a walkway in 2009.

  • Colors and teams

    The school colors are green and blue, and the teams are the Green Wave. The mascot is Riptide the Pelican.

    Howard-Tilton Memorial Library

    Freret St at Newcomb Pl, New Orleans, LA 70118

    Tulane's main library moved into its current building on Freret Street in 1968. It holds the Hogan Jazz Archive and the Latin American Library. Hurricane Katrina flooded its basement, and a 2013–2016 project added two floors.

4 traditions to check off

Stamped! keeps these as a checklist, so you can tick each one off on the visit.

  • Ride the St. Charles Streetcar

    The St. Charles line has run since 1835 and is the oldest continuously operating streetcar line in the world. Ride it to the Tulane University stop in front of Gibson Hall.

  • Tiffany Windows on Woodward Way

    Two Louis Comfort Tiffany stained-glass triptychs, made in the 1890s for a chapel at Newcomb College's old Garden District campus, now line the Woodward Way breezeway at the Newcomb Art Museum. Stop and look for them.

  • Rogers Memorial Chapel

    More of Tulane's Tiffany stained glass is in the Myra Clare Rogers Memorial Chapel on the Newcomb side of campus. Peek inside to see the windows.

  • Maya Gallery in Dinwiddie Hall

    The Middle American Research Institute has studied the Maya since 1924. Its gallery on the third floor of Dinwiddie Hall is open to the public on weekdays.

History

Founded 1834

From Wikipedia

The institution became private under the endowments of Paul Tulane and Josephine Louise Newcomb in 1884 and 1887. The Tulane University School of Law and the Tulane University School of Medicine are, respectively, the 12th oldest law school and 15th oldest medical school in the United States.
