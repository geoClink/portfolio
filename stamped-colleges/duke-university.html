Planning the visit

  • Fly intoRaleigh-Durham International Airport (RDU)

    Raleigh-Durham International, east of Durham, is the main airport for the Research Triangle.

  • Getting aroundWalking / Duke Transit

    Each campus is walkable. Duke's free campus buses, including the C1 East-West route, link East and West campuses.

  • Colors and teams

    The school colors are Duke blue and white, and the teams are the Blue Devils.

5 buildings to stamp

  1. 01

    Duke Chapel

    West Campus, Duke University, Durham, NC 27708

    Finished in 1935, the chapel was the last of the original West Campus buildings and rises 210 feet at the center of campus. Julian Abele designed it in Collegiate Gothic stone for about 1,800 worshippers.

  3. 03

    Cameron Indoor Stadium

    115 Whitford Dr, Durham, NC 27708

    Julian Abele's first drawings signed under his own name were for this 9,314-seat arena, which opened in 1940. It is the home of Duke basketball and its Cameron Crazies.

    Built
    1940
    Architect

  4. 04

    J. Deryl Hart House

    2324 Duke University Rd, Durham, NC 27708

    This three-story brick and half-timbered Tudor Revival house was built in 1933–1934 as one of five faculty homes on early West Campus. It is now the official residence of Duke's president.

  5. 05

    Wallace Wade Stadium

    Frank Bassett Dr, Durham, NC 27708

    Horace Trumbauer's firm designed the football stadium, which opened in 1929 in a wooded hollow on West Campus. It seats about 35,000 for Blue Devils football.

2 traditions to check off

Stamped! keeps these as a checklist, so you can tick each one off on the visit.

  • Duke Chapel Carillon

    The 50-bell carillon in the Duke Chapel tower is played every weekday at 5 p.m., and the alma mater is played on Fridays. Visitors gather near the chapel to listen.

  • Krzyzewskiville

    Before the biggest men's basketball games, students camp in a tent city called Krzyzewskiville on the lawn beside Cameron Indoor Stadium. Visitors in winter can walk past the tents.
