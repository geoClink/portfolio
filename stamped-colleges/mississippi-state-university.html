Planning the visit

  • Fly intoGolden Triangle Regional Airport (GTR)

    Golden Triangle Regional Airport, east of Starkville, serves the area.

  • Getting aroundWalking / SMART buses

    The campus around the Drill Field is walkable. Starkville–MSU Area Rapid Transit runs free buses on campus and in town.

  • Colors and teams

    The school colors are maroon and white, and the teams are the Bulldogs.

    Montgomery Hall

    Old Main Plaza, Mississippi State, MS 39762

    R. H. Hunt designed this Beaux-Arts science building, built in 1902–1903. Corinthian columns mark its central pavilion, and a five-story apse projects from the rear.

    Mitchell Memorial Library

    395 Hardy Rd, Mississippi State, MS 39762

    Built in 1950 on the east side of the Drill Field, this is the largest building on the quadrangle and the main university library. It holds the Ulysses S. Grant Presidential Library.

  4. 04

    Davis Wade Stadium

    90 BS Hood Dr, Mississippi State, MS 39762

    Built in 1914 as New Athletic Field, this is the second-oldest stadium in the Football Bowl Subdivision. Its 1936 concrete stands were built with WPA labor.

2 traditions to check off

Stamped! keeps these as a checklist, so you can tick each one off on the visit.

  • Cowbells at Davis Wade Stadium

    Fans ring cowbells at Mississippi State games, the most famous symbol of the school's spirit. Bring a cowbell to a home game, or hear them at the Cowbell Yell pep rally in the stadium.

  • The Drill Field

    The main quadrangle is named for its use by the Corps of Cadets. Students relax here between classes, and the Drill pep rally is held on it in the first week of classes.

Mississippi State University for Agriculture and Applied Science, commonly known as Mississippi State University (MSU), is a public land-grant research university in Mississippi State, Mississippi, United States.
