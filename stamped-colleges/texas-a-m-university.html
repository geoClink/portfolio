Planning the visit

  • Fly intoEasterwood Airport (CLL)

    Easterwood Airport, at the west edge of College Station near campus, has American Eagle flights.

  • Getting aroundWalking / Texas A&M buses

    Main Campus is walkable. Texas A&M runs campus bus routes during the semester.

  • Colors and teams

    The school colors are maroon and white, and the teams are the Aggies.

    Old Main Dr, College Station, TX 77843

    Given by Martha and Ford D. Albritton and dedicated in 1984, the 42-meter tower holds 49 bells cast by the Paccard foundry. The bells ring every quarter-hour.

    Cushing Memorial Library

    Nagle St, College Station, TX 77843

    Built in 1930 as the first freestanding library on campus, this neoclassical building by F. E. Giesecke and S. C. P. Vosper has pilasters carved with ram heads and cow skulls. It now holds special collections and the University Archives.

2 traditions to check off

Stamped! keeps these as a checklist, so you can tick each one off on the visit.

  • Pennies on Sully

    Students leave pennies at the base of the statue of Lawrence Sullivan Ross for luck before exams, recalling his reply "a penny for your thoughts." The coins are given to charity each semester.

  • Century Tree

    This live oak, one of the first trees planted on campus, is well over 100 years old. By legend, a couple who walk beneath it together will marry.
