Rice University

When Rice opened in 1912, students paid no tuition at all, as founder William Marsh Rice's will and the institute's first charter required.

Planning the visit

  • Fly intoWilliam P. Hobby Airport (HOU)

    Hobby is the closer of Houston's two main airports to Rice. George Bush Intercontinental (IAH) has many more long-haul flights but is a longer drive north.

  • Getting aroundWalking / METRORail Red Line

    The wooded campus is easy to walk. The METRORail Red Line runs along Main Street, with the Hermann Park/Rice U station near the campus's eastern corner.

  • Colors and teams

    The mascot is Sammy the Owl: when Texas A&M students stole the owl mascot in 1917, a detective's coded telegram reported that 'Sammy is fairly well.' The colors are blue and gray.

5 traditions to check off

Stamped! keeps these as a checklist, so you can tick each one off on the visit.

  • Through the Sallyport

    By tradition, Rice students pass through the Sallyport arch in Lovett Hall as they leave at graduation and avoid it until then. Stand under the arch and look across the Academic Quad.

  • Skyspace at Sunset

    James Turrell's Twilight Epiphany Skyspace puts on a free light show at sunrise and sunset, as changing colored light transforms the sky seen through its roof opening. Arrive early for a seat.

  • The Moon Speech Stadium

    In 1962 President Kennedy spoke at Rice Stadium and challenged the nation to reach the Moon by the end of the decade. Visit the stadium where the famous speech was given.

  • The Marching Owl Band

    The Marching Owl Band, or MOB, is a scatter band: instead of marching in formation, it performs comic skits at football games. Catch its halftime show.

  • A Tree for Every Student

    More than 4,000 trees and shrubs make the whole campus an arboretum, which gave rise to the legend that Rice has a tree for every student. Stroll the shady quads.

History

Founded 1891

From Wikipedia

William Marsh Rice University, also known as Rice University, is a private research university in Houston, Texas, United States. Established in 1912, it spans 300 acres. The university consists of eight constituent schools in the fields of architecture, business, continuing studies, engineering, humanities, music, natural sciences, and social sciences.
