Planning the visit

  • Fly intoSalt Lake City International Airport (SLC)

    Salt Lake City International, north of Provo, is the main airport for Utah.

  • Getting aroundWalking / UTA UVX

    The campus is walkable. UTA's UVX bus rapid transit line has four stops at BYU and links it with Utah Valley University and the FrontRunner stations in Provo and Orem.

  • Colors and teams

    The school colors are navy blue and white, and the teams are the Cougars.

4 buildings to stamp

  1. 01

    Centennial Carillon Tower

    Brigham Young University campus, Provo, UT

    Fred L. Markham designed this simple modern bell tower, dedicated in October 1975 for the school's 100th anniversary. Its carillon was built with 53 bells, the largest weighing 4,730 pounds, and a 54th bell was added in 2025.

  2. 02

    Museum of Art

    Brigham Young University campus, Provo, UT

    Opened in October 1993, the museum was built to show BYU's collection of more than 17,000 works, which had never had a permanent home. It is one of the best-attended university art museums in the United States.

  3. 03

    Spencer W. Kimball Tower

    Brigham Young University campus, Provo, UT

    Completed in 1981, the 12-story tower holds classrooms and offices. It was set at a 45-degree angle to the buildings around it to avoid a corridor effect.

    Brigham Young University campus, Provo, UT

3 traditions to check off

Stamped! keeps these as a checklist, so you can tick each one off on the visit.

  • Brigham Young Statue

    A statue of Brigham Young by his grandson, sculptor Mahonri Young, stands in front of the Abraham O. Smoot Administration Building. Visit the statue of the man the school is named for.

  • Stadium Cougar Statue

    A bronze cougar, cast in Italy and given by the classes of 1965 and 1969, guards LaVell Edwards Stadium on the northeast end of campus. Take a photo with it.

  • Hourly Carillon Tune

    At the top of every hour the Centennial Carillon tolls the first lines of a tune based on the hymn "Come, Come, Ye Saints." Stand near the tower on the hour to hear it.

History

Founded 1875

From Wikipedia

Brigham Young University (BYU) is a private research university in Provo, Utah, United States. It was founded in 1875 by religious leader Brigham Young and is the flagship university of the Church Educational System (CES) of the Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints.
