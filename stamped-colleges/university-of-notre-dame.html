Home of the Fighting Irish

University of Notre Dame

The Golden Dome, topped by a statue of Mary, rises over a campus of around 170 buildings set around two lakes and seven quadrangles, often ranked among the most beautiful in the world.

Planning the visit

  • Fly intoSouth Bend International Airport (SBN)

    South Bend International is a short drive from campus. The South Shore Line commuter train runs from the airport to downtown Chicago.

  • Getting aroundWalking

    The historic core around the Main Quad and South Quad is best explored on foot, with the Basilica, the Dome and the Grotto all a few minutes apart.

  • Colors and teams

    Campus has its own vocabulary: the Main Quad is 'God Quad', and the Word of Life mural on the Hesburgh Library is better known as 'Touchdown Jesus'.

5 traditions to check off

Stamped! keeps these as a checklist, so you can tick each one off on the visit.

  • Main Building Front Steps

    Undergraduates avoid the front steps of the Main Building, as legend says using them means not graduating; seniors first walk them at graduation. Visitors are welcome to use the steps and take a photo under the Golden Dome.

  • Light a Candle at the Grotto

    Students, alumni and visitors light candles and say a prayer at the Grotto of Our Lady of Lourdes; Notre Dame Magazine jokes you should leave double the suggested offering "just to be sure." Visitors can light a candle and read Tom Dooley's letter to Father Hesburgh there.

  • Touchdown Jesus

    The Word of Life mural on the face of the Hesburgh Library, better known as Touchdown Jesus, is one of the campus landmarks where visiting families line up for photos. Visitors can pose in front of it by the reflecting pool.

  • Fair Catch Corby

    The statue of Father William Corby with his arm raised, nicknamed Fair-Catch Corby, stands outside Corby Hall and is a classic campus photo stop. Visitors can pose beside it.

  • Lyons Hall Arch

    Campus superstition holds that a couple who kiss under the archway of Lyons Hall may get engaged before graduation. Visitors can walk through the arch and see it for themselves.
