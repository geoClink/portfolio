Planning the visit

  • Fly intoLincoln Airport (LNK)

    Lincoln Airport, northwest of downtown, is the nearest airport. Omaha's Eppley Airfield has more flights.

  • Getting aroundWalking / StarTran

    StarTran buses link City, East and Innovation campuses, and students get a bus pass through their transit fee.

  • Colors and teams

    The school colors are scarlet and cream, and the teams are the Cornhuskers.

4 buildings to stamp

  1. 01

    Sheldon Museum of Art

    451 N 12th St, Lincoln, NE 68588

    Philip Johnson designed this modern museum of Italian travertine, opened in 1963. Its grand hall and central staircase lead to collections of American art.

  2. 02

    Architecture Hall

    11th and R Streets, Lincoln, NE 68588

    Mendelssohn, Fisher and Lawrie designed this Richardsonian Romanesque library, built in 1895. It is the oldest building on campus and now houses the College of Architecture.

    Built
    1895
    Architect
    Mendelssohn, Fisher and Lawrie
    Style
    Richardsonian Romanesque
    Today
    College of Architecture
  3. 03

    Mueller Tower

    City Campus, University of Nebraska–Lincoln, Lincoln, NE 68588

    This stone carillon tower, built in 1949, was named for alumnus and donor Ralph S. Mueller. A restoration begun in 2025 scanned and catalogued each of its 1,700 stones.

    Built
    1949
    Architect
    George Kuska Jr. (Meginnis and Schaumberg)
    Style
    Art Deco
    Height
    26 m (85 ft)
    Today
    Carillon Tower
  4. 04

    Memorial Stadium

    600 Stadium Dr, Lincoln, NE 68588

    Opened in 1923, the stadium honors Nebraskans who served in the nation's wars. Its architects, John Latenser Sr. and Ellery L. Davis, worked without pay.

    Built
    1923
    Architect
    John Latenser Sr. and Ellery L. Davis
    Today
    Football Stadium

1 tradition to check off

Stamped! keeps these as a checklist, so you can tick each one off on the visit.

  • Archie the Mammoth

    Students rub the foot of Archie, the bronze mammoth outside Morrill Hall, for luck before tests and projects. Give his foot a rub or a high-five.
