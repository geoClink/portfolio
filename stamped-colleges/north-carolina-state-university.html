Planning the visit

  • Fly intoRaleigh-Durham International Airport (RDU)

    Raleigh-Durham International, northwest of Raleigh, is the main airport for the Research Triangle.

  • Getting aroundWalking / Wolfline

    North Campus is walkable. The fare-free Wolfline buses connect Main and Centennial campuses.

  • Colors and teams

    The school colors are red and white, and the teams are the Wolfpack.

4 buildings to stamp

  1. 01

    Memorial Belltower

    Raleigh, NC 27695

    This 115-foot granite tower honors alumni killed in World War I. Its shaft mixes Romanesque features with Gothic height, and the tower was formally dedicated in 1949.

    Built
    1937
    Architect
    William Henry Deacy
    Style

    Height

  2. 02

    D. H. Hill Jr. Library

    Raleigh, NC 27695

    The main North Campus library was completed in 1953 and opened in 1954, then grew in several later stages into a tower on Hillsborough Street. It holds most of the university's books.

    Built
    1954
    Architect

    Today

  3. 03

    Gregg Museum of Art & Design

    1903 Hillsborough St, Raleigh, NC 27607

    Hobart Upjohn designed this Georgian Revival house, finished in 1928, as the chancellor's residence. It now houses the university art museum, with a new wing by Perkins and Will.

    Built
    1928
    Architect
    Hobart Upjohn; Perkins and Will (addition)
    Style
    Georgian Revival
    Today
    Art Museum
  4. 04

    Reynolds Coliseum

    103 Dunn Ave, Raleigh, NC 27607

    William Neal Reynolds Coliseum opened in 1949 for agricultural shows and basketball. It hosts women's basketball, volleyball and ROTC programs.

    Built
    1949
    Today
    Arena and ROTC

2 traditions to check off

Stamped! keeps these as a checklist, so you can tick each one off on the visit.

  • Belltower Lit Red

    When NC State wins a major sporting event, the Memorial Belltower is lit red. Fans gather at its base to chant and make the Wolfpack hand sign.

  • Free Expression Tunnel

    Anyone is allowed to paint the walls, ceiling and floor of this pedestrian tunnel under the railroad between North and Central Campus. Visitors walk through to see the latest messages.

History

Founded 1887

From Wikipedia

North Carolina State University is a public land-grant research university in Raleigh, North Carolina, United States. Founded in 1887 by the North Carolina General Assembly as an agricultural and mechanical college, it is the largest university in the Carolinas and a constituent institution of the University of North Carolina system.
