    Main Library

    366 W Circle Dr, East Lansing, MI 48824

    Completed in 1955 on the north bank of the Red Cedar River, overlooking Beal Botanical Garden, this research library replaced the old one, which became the MSU Museum, and students lined up to pass the books across. An east wing added in 1967 tripled its shelf space, and it holds one of the world's largest comic art collections and the Vincent Voice Library.

10 traditions to check off

Stamped! keeps these as a checklist, so you can tick each one off on the visit.

  • The Rock

    Given by the Class of 1873, the Rock beside the Red Cedar River is painted over again and again with new messages. Stop to see what it says today.

  • The Spartan Statue

    The nearly 10-foot bronze Spartan is a favorite photo backdrop, especially at graduation. Take a selfie with it.

  • Beaumont Tower Open Tower

    Beaumont Tower opens to visitors on the first Wednesday of each month and at graduation. Go up to see the carillon and the view over campus.

  • MSU Dairy Store

    The MSU Dairy Store was voted Grand Champion in the American Dairy Science Association's 2025 Ice Cream Competition. Stop at its Anthony Hall shop and try a flavor.

  • W. J. Beal Botanical Garden

    Professor William J. Beal began this garden in 1873, and it is claimed to be the oldest continuously maintained university botanical garden in the US. Stroll it for free any day during daylight hours.

  • MSU Horticulture Gardens

    The gardens on Bogue Street hold more than 700 rose cultivars and the Michigan 4-H Children's Garden. Wander them for free any day.

  • Red Cedar River Trail

    A trail runs across campus along the south bank of the Red Cedar River, and the Lansing River Trail starts here. Bike or walk it, or kayak the river.

  • The Izzone

    Named for coach Tom Izzo, the Izzone is the student section of about 5,000 at men's basketball games in the Breslin Center. Catch a game to hear it roar.

  • Magic Johnson Statue

    A 12-foot bronze of Earvin "Magic" Johnson, who led MSU to the 1979 national title, has stood outside the Breslin Center since 2003. Snap a photo with it.

  • Spartan Marching Band March

    On football Saturdays the band warms up on Adams Field, then marches past the Spartan Statue and up Red Cedar Road to the stadium about an hour before kickoff. Line the route to cheer it on.
