Planning the visit

  • Fly intoCapital Region International Airport (LAN)

    Capital Region International Airport in Lansing is the nearest airport with airline service.

  • Getting aroundWalking / CATA buses

    CATA buses run routes through campus and connect it with East Lansing and Lansing.

  • Colors and teams

    The school colors are green and white, and the teams are the Spartans.

4 buildings to stamp

  1. 01

    Beaumont Tower

    Michigan State University, East Lansing, MI 48824

    Donaldson and Meier designed this Collegiate Gothic tower, completed in 1928 on the site of College Hall, the first building on campus. It was given by alumnus John W. Beaumont as a monument to teaching.

  3. 03

    Cowles House

    W Circle Dr, East Lansing, MI 48824

    Built in 1857 to house faculty, this is the oldest surviving building on campus, though only its foundation and two outer walls are original. It has been the official president's residence since 1941.

    Built
    1857
    Architect
    J. J. Scott (1857); Calder (1950)
    Style
    Eclectic
    Today
    President's House
  4. 04

    MSU Union

    Michigan State University, East Lansing, MI 48824

    Chicago firm Pond and Pond designed this Collegiate Gothic student union, built from 1923 to 1925. It has arched openings, gables and tall windows.

    1925
    Architect

3 traditions to check off

Stamped! keeps these as a checklist, so you can tick each one off on the visit.

  • The Rock

    Given by the Class of 1873, the Rock beside the Red Cedar River is painted over again and again with new messages. Stop to see what it says today.

  • The Spartan Statue

    The nearly 10-foot bronze Spartan is a favorite photo backdrop, especially at graduation. Take a selfie with it.

  • Beaumont Tower Open Tower

    Beaumont Tower opens to visitors on the first Wednesday of each month and at graduation. Go up to see the carillon and the view over campus.
