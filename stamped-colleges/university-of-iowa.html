University of Iowa

The Greek Revival Old Capitol, Iowa's first state capitol building, stands at the center of the Pentacrest, ringed by four Beaux-Arts halls.

Planning the visit

  • Fly intoThe Eastern Iowa Airport (CID)

    The Eastern Iowa Airport, near Cedar Rapids to the north of Iowa City, is the nearest airport with airline service.

  • Getting aroundWalking / Cambus

    The university's free Cambus service runs routes across campus and to the hospitals.

  • Colors and teams

    The school colors are black and gold, and the teams are the Hawkeyes.

4 buildings to stamp

  1. 01

    Old Capitol

    Pentacrest, University of Iowa, Iowa City, IA 52242

    John F. Rague designed this Greek Revival building, begun in 1842 as the main government building of the state of Iowa. It is now the central landmark of the campus and a National Historic Landmark.

    Architect

  2. 02

    Macbride Hall

    17 N Clinton St, Iowa City, IA 52242

    Proudfoot & Bird designed this Beaux-Arts hall, completed in 1908 as the Natural Sciences Building on the Pentacrest. It houses the university's Museum of Natural History.

    Built
    1908

    Today

  3. 03

    Stanley Museum of Art

    Gibson Square, University of Iowa, Iowa City, IA 52242

    BNIM Iowa, led by Rod Kruse, designed this brick museum, opened in 2022 to replace the building lost to the 2008 flood. It sits above the flood plain and holds Jackson Pollock's Mural.

    Built
    2022
    Architect
    BNIM Iowa (Rod Kruse)
    Style
    Rectilinear Brick
    Today
    Art Museum
  4. 04

    Voxman Music Building

    93 E Burlington St, Iowa City, IA 52242

    LMN Architects and Neumann Monson designed this six-story music school, opened in October 2016. It replaced the original Voxman building on the Iowa River, destroyed in the 2008 flood.

2 traditions to check off

Stamped! keeps these as a checklist, so you can tick each one off on the visit.

  • Old Capitol Photo

    Graduates like to pose in front of the Old Capitol, by its columns and the doorway with "Iowa" above it. Take your own photo on the steps at the heart of the Pentacrest.

  • Kinnick Wave

    At home football games, after the first quarter the whole crowd turns and waves to the patients watching from the children's hospital above the east stands. If you go to a game, join in.
