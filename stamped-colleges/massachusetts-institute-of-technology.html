Mens et Manus

Massachusetts Institute of Technology

MIT names nearly all its buildings by number, so the Great Dome is Building 10 and Frank Gehry's Stata Center is Building 32, standing on the site of the legendary Building 20 that housed the wartime Radiation Laboratory.

Planning the visit

  • Fly intoBoston Logan International Airport (BOS)

    Logan is about 7 km from MIT across the Charles River. The MBTA links the airport to Kendall/MIT station on the Red Line.

  • Getting aroundWalking / MBTA Red Line

    The campus runs along the Charles River and is easy to walk end to end. Kendall/MIT station on the Red Line sits at its eastern edge.

  • Colors and teams

    At MIT you ask for directions by number: rooms are written like 'W16-100', building then room.

    Kresge Auditorium

    48 Massachusetts Ave, Cambridge, MA 02139

    Eero Saarinen's thin-shell concrete dome is one-eighth of a sphere, proportionately thinner than an eggshell, resting on just three points with glass curtain walls filling the gaps. It was dedicated in 1955.

  3. 03

    MIT Chapel

    Kresge Oval, Cambridge, MA 02139

    From outside, a windowless brick cylinder in a shallow moat; inside, light rippling up from the water and a shimmering metal screen by Harry Bertoia falling from a skylight onto a plain marble altar. Saarinen designed it as a companion to Kresge Auditorium.

    Green Building

    21 Ames St, Cambridge, MA 02139

2 traditions to check off

Stamped! keeps these as a checklist, so you can tick each one off on the visit.

  • MIThenge

    On a few days around mid-November and late January, the setting sun lines up with the Infinite Corridor and shines down its whole length. Crowds gather in the corridor to watch the light pour through.

  • Smoot markers on the Harvard Bridge

    The Harvard Bridge sidewalk is painted with marks measuring its length in 'smoots', the height of 1958 pledge Oliver Smoot. Walk the bridge to find the 'Halfway to Hell' mark and the final '364.4 smoots plus or minus one ear'.
