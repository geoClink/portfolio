Planning the visit

  • Fly intoHartsfield–Jackson Atlanta International Airport (ATL)

    Athens has no scheduled airline service, so most visitors fly into Atlanta's Hartsfield–Jackson airport, southwest of town.

  • Getting aroundWalking / UGA Campus Transit

    North Campus is walkable. UGA Campus Transit buses run routes across campus.

  • Colors and teams

    The school colors are red and black, and the teams are the Bulldogs.

4 buildings to stamp

  1. 01

    Demosthenian Hall

    North Campus, University of Georgia, Athens, GA 30602

    Built in 1824 for one of the nation's oldest student debating societies, this North Campus hall is still used for weekly meetings. Its upper chamber keeps a plaster ceiling medallion based on a design by Asher Benjamin.

  2. 02

    President's House

    570 Prince Ave, Athens, GA 30601

    John Thomas Grant built this Greek Revival mansion in 1856. It was given to the university in 1949 and has been the president's residence since.

    Built
    1856
    Style

  3. 03

    Lucy Cobb Institute

    200 N Milledge Ave, Athens, GA 30601

    Built in 1858 for a girls' school founded by Thomas R. R. Cobb, the building closed as a school in 1931. It now houses the university's Carl Vinson Institute of Government.

    Built
    1858

  4. 04

    Seney–Stovall Chapel

    N Milledge Ave, Athens, GA 30601

    This octagonal red brick chapel on the Lucy Cobb grounds was funded after a student wrote to George I. Seney asking for help. Local architect William Winstead Thomas designed it, and it was dedicated in 1885.

2 traditions to check off

Stamped! keeps these as a checklist, so you can tick each one off on the visit.

  • The Arch

    The Arch is the gateway between North Campus and downtown Athens. By legend, students do not walk under it until they graduate, so many visitors walk around it and take a photo instead.

  • Chapel Bell

    The bell beside the Chapel has been rung to celebrate football wins since 1894. The university encourages ringing it for athletic victories and other special occasions.

History

Founded 1785

From Wikipedia

The University of Georgia is a public land-grant, sea-grant, and space-grant research university with its main campus in Athens, Georgia, United States. Chartered in 1785, it is the first state-chartered public university in the United States. It is the flagship university of the University System of Georgia.
