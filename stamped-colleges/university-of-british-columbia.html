Point Grey Campus

University of British Columbia

UBC's Brock Commons Tallwood House was the tallest mass timber building in the world when it opened in 2017, and Arthur Erickson's Museum of Anthropology recreates the post-and-beam houses of Northwest Coast First Nations in concrete.

Planning the visit

  • Fly intoVancouver International Airport (YVR)

    YVR is on Sea Island in Richmond, south of the campus. The Canada Line takes you into Vancouver, where buses continue west to the UBC Exchange.

  • Getting aroundBus / Walking

    Frequent buses run from downtown Vancouver and the Canada Line to the UBC Exchange in the centre of campus. From there, Main Mall leads on foot to most of the buildings.

  • Colors and teams

    The campus sits on Point Grey, and it is not officially part of the City of Vancouver, even though everyone thinks of it as Vancouver.

3 traditions to check off

Stamped! keeps these as a checklist, so you can tick each one off on the visit.

  • The Great Trek Cairn

    This stone cairn on Main Mall marks the 1922 Great Trek, when students marched to Point Grey to push the government to finish the campus, and it was built from stones gathered on site. A ceremony is held there each September during Homecoming, and visitors can read its inscription any time.

  • The Painted Engineering Cairn

    Engineering students look after a concrete monument on Main Mall that is repainted for causes, anniversaries and campus events. Visitors can stop by to see its latest paint job.

  • Ladner Clock Tower Chimes

    The Ladner Clock Tower in front of the Main Library chimes on the hour, and its carillon plays short sets during the day and on special occasions such as convocation. Visitors can listen from the Library Garden.

From Wikipedia

The University of British Columbia (UBC) is a public research university with two main campuses near Vancouver and Kelowna, in British Columbia, Canada. With an annual research budget of $893 million, UBC funds 9,992 projects annually in various fields of study within the industrial sector, as well as governmental and non-governmental organizations.
