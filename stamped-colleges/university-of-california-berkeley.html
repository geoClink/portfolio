Cal

University of California, Berkeley

John Galen Howard shaped the heart of the campus with a Beaux-Arts plan. He designed the Greek Theatre, the Hearst Mining Building, Sather Gate and the Campanile, which is one of the tallest bell-and-clock towers in the world.

Planning the visit

  • Fly intoOakland International Airport (OAK)

    Oakland's airport is the closest to campus. BART trains link both Oakland and San Francisco International to Downtown Berkeley station, a short walk from Sather Gate.

  • Getting aroundWalking / BART

    Downtown Berkeley BART station sits a few blocks west of the campus. Inside the campus, everything from Sather Gate to the Greek Theatre is within walking distance.

  • Colors and teams

    Locals call the university simply 'Cal', and Sather Tower is known to everyone as 'the Campanile'.

6 buildings to stamp

  1. 01

    Sather Tower

    Sather Tower, UC Berkeley, Berkeley, CA 94720

    Known to everyone as the Campanile, this 307-foot clock tower was modelled on St Mark's Campanile in Venice. Its 61-bell carillon can be heard across the city, and its cool, dry interior stores fossils from the La Brea Tar Pits.

    Built
    1914
    Architect
    John Galen Howard
    Style
    Gothic Revival
    Height
    94 m (308 ft)
    Today
    Bell tower, observation deck and fossil storage

    Hearst Greek Theatre

    2001 Gayley Rd, Berkeley, CA 94720

4 traditions to check off

Stamped! keeps these as a checklist, so you can tick each one off on the visit.

  • The Campanile

    Ride the elevator to the observation deck of Sather Tower for views of campus and the Bay. The carillon plays short daily concerts, and on the last day of classes it plays "They're Hanging Danny Deever in the Morning" before going silent for finals.

  • The 4.0 Ball

    Students rub or kiss the stone ball in front of the Campanile for good luck, especially after stepping on a University seal. Give it a touch on your way past.

  • Athena at Doe Library

    Legend says walking under the statue of Athena at the Doe Library entrance gives you wisdom, but walking back out under her takes it away. Students leave by the back doors to keep it.

  • Hike to the Big C

    A walk from near the Foothill parking area leads up to the Big C on the hillside above campus, a hike almost every Cal student makes. Enjoy the view back over campus and the Bay.

History

Founded 1868

From Wikipedia

The University of California, Berkeley is a public land-grant research university in Berkeley, California, United States. Founded in 1868 and named after the Anglo-Irish philosopher and clergyman George Berkeley, it is the state's first land-grant university and the founding campus of the University of California system.
