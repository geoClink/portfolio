Yale University

Yale's central campus is the finest collection of Collegiate Gothic in America: most of it was designed by James Gamble Rogers in the 1920s and 1930s, with acid poured on the stone and windows deliberately cracked to make the new buildings look centuries old.

Planning the visit

  • Fly intoBradley International Airport (BDL)

    Bradley, near Hartford, is about an hour's drive north of New Haven. Many visitors also fly into New York and take Metro-North's New Haven Line straight to Union Station.

  • Getting aroundWalking / Yale Shuttle

    The central campus is compact and best seen on foot. The free Yale Shuttle loops between the colleges, the Science Hill and the medical campus.

  • Colors and teams

    Campus life has its own vocabulary: undergraduates belong to one of fourteen residential colleges, and the bell tower everyone meets under is simply 'Harkness'.

    Yale University Art Gallery

    1111 Chapel St, New Haven, CT 06510

    The oldest university art museum in the Western Hemisphere. Its modernist main building, finished in 1953, was one of Louis Kahn's first works and the commission that transformed his career; it links to the older 1928 gallery and Gothic Street Hall.

3 traditions to check off

Stamped! keeps these as a checklist, so you can tick each one off on the visit.

  • Rub Woolsey's Foot

    Generations of students have rubbed the protruding foot of the bronze statue of President Theodore Dwight Woolsey on Old Campus for good luck. Visitors can give the shiny foot a rub too.

  • Hear the Harkness Carillon

    Students in the Yale Guild of Carillonneurs play the carillon in Harkness Tower twice a day during the school year, at 12:30 and 5:30 p.m., plus Friday concerts in summer. Visitors can listen from the streets and courtyards nearby.

  • Visit the Women's Table

    Maya Lin's 1993 Women's Table in front of Sterling Memorial Library is carved with a spiral of numbers showing how many women studied at Yale each year since 1701. Visitors can walk up and read the spiral.

History

Founded 1701

From Wikipedia

Yale University is a private Ivy League research university in New Haven, Connecticut, United States. Founded in 1701, Yale is the fourth oldest institution of higher education in the United States. It is one of the nine colonial colleges chartered before the American Revolution.
