Planning the visit

  • Fly intoPittsburgh International Airport (PIT)

    Pittsburgh International, west of the city, is the main airport for the region.

  • Getting aroundWalking / Pitt shuttles

    The Oakland campus is walkable. Pitt runs its own shuttles around Oakland, and students ride Pittsburgh Regional Transit buses, light rail and inclines free with their Pitt ID.

  • Colors and teams

    The school colors are blue and gold, and the teams are the Panthers.

    Forbes Ave, Pittsburgh, PA 15260

3 traditions to check off

Stamped! keeps these as a checklist, so you can tick each one off on the visit.

  • Victory Lights

    The top of the Cathedral of Learning glows gold after Pitt football wins and other championships, with a blue beam added in 2018. Visitors can see it across Oakland.

  • Panther Statue

    The panther statue at the William Pitt Union has a worn nose from years of students rubbing it for good luck. Visitors rub it too.

  • Heinz Chapel Steps

    The steps in front of Heinz Memorial Chapel are said to be a good luck charm for young couples. Visitors take a photo on them.

History

Founded 1787

From Wikipedia

The 132-acre Pittsburgh campus includes various historic buildings that are part of the Schenley Farms Historic District, most notably its 42-story Gothic revival centerpiece, the Cathedral of Learning. Pitt is a member of the Association of American Universities and is classified among "R1: Doctoral Universities – Very high research activity".
