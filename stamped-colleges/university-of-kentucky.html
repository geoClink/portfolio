Planning the visit

  • Fly intoBlue Grass Airport (LEX)

    Blue Grass Airport is just west of Lexington.

  • Getting aroundWalking / Lextran buses

    Central campus is walkable. UK's campus bus routes, run with Lextran, are free, and students and staff ride Lextran city buses free with a UK ID.

  • Colors and teams

    The school colors are blue and white, and the teams are the Wildcats.

5 buildings to stamp

  1. 01

    Main Building

    Main Building, University of Kentucky, Lexington, KY 40506

    Dedicated in 1882, this brick and stone building is the only one left of the four that first housed State College. It was rebuilt inside after a 2001 fire and reopened in 2004.

  2. 02

    Memorial Hall

    610 S Limestone, Lexington, KY 40506

    Completed in 1929 as a memorial to those who died in World War I, this 40-meter hall with Corinthian columns and a spire was paid for by a ten-year subscription campaign. Its lobby holds a 1934 fresco by Ann Rice O'Hanlon.

    Built
    1929
    Architect
    Warner and Mitchell
    Style
    Colonial Revival
    Height
    40 m (131 ft)
    Today
    Auditorium
  3. 03

    William T. Young Library

    500 S Limestone, Lexington, KY 40506

    Kallmann McKinnell & Wood of Boston designed this six-story library, dedicated in 1998. Its octagonal plan has two-story brick-and-stone arcades on each facade.

  4. 04

    Margaret I. King Library

    179 Funkhouser Dr, Lexington, KY 40506

    The original library was completed in 1931 for 250,000 volumes, with additions in 1963 and 1974. It now houses special collections.

    Built
    1931
    Today
    Special Collections Library
  5. 05

    Patterson Office Tower

    Patterson Office Tower, University of Kentucky, Lexington, KY 40506

    Built from 1967 to 1969 and named for the university's first president, James Kennedy Patterson, this tower holds offices for about 960 faculty and staff.

    Built

1 tradition to check off

Stamped! keeps these as a checklist, so you can tick each one off on the visit.

  • Bowman the Wildcat

    The bronze Wildcat statue in Wildcat Alumni Plaza was named Bowman by student vote in 2012. Visitors pat his head and rub his nose and paws for good luck.
