New York University

Most of NYU's buildings are scattered around Washington Square Park in Greenwich Village. Samuel Morse worked on the telegraph in the university's first Main Building, on the site of today's Silver Center.

Planning the visit

  • Fly intoLaGuardia Airport (LGA)

    New York City has three major airports: LaGuardia, JFK and Newark. AirTrain links JFK and Newark with trains into Manhattan.

  • Getting aroundWalking / NYC Subway

    The West Fourth Street–Washington Square station is just west of the park at Sixth Avenue, and the 8th Street–NYU station on Broadway serves the east side of campus.

  • Colors and teams

    The school colors are NYU Violet and white, and the teams are the Violets. The mascot, since 1981, is a bobcat.

  1. 01

    Silver Center

    32 Waverly Pl, New York, NY

    Alfred Zucker designed this brick, stone and terra-cotta building in 1892 to replace NYU's Gothic Revival first building on the same spot. Known as Main Building until 2002, it is home to the College of Arts and Science.

    Brown Building

    23–29 Washington Pl, New York, NY

    John Woolley designed this ten-story iron and steel loft building, completed in 1901. Built as the Asch Building, it was the site of the 1911 Triangle Shirtwaist Factory fire and is now a National Historic Landmark owned by NYU.

    Provincetown Playhouse

    133 MacDougal St, New York, NY

    The Provincetown Players, whose members included Eugene O'Neill, turned a former stable and wine-bottling plant into this theater in 1918. Now owned by NYU, it is used by the Steinhardt School.

4 traditions to check off

Stamped! keeps these as a checklist, so you can tick each one off on the visit.

  • Washington Square Arch

    The arch belongs to the city, but it has become the unofficial symbol of NYU, whose buildings ring the park. Take a photo with the arch and Fifth Avenue behind it.

  • Bust of Sylvette

    In the plaza between the Silver Towers stands a huge enlargement of Picasso's Bust of Sylvette, made with Carl Nesjar. Walk into the courtyard to see it up close.

  • Gould Plaza

    This plaza off West 4th Street, framed by the Stern School and the Courant Institute, is where NYU students gather after classes. Look for the purple NYU flags on the buildings around it.

  • Grey Art Museum

    NYU's fine art museum, known as the Grey Art Gallery until 2023, now has larger galleries at 18 Cooper Square. Visit to see the university's collection, including modern Asian and Middle Eastern art.

History

Founded 1831

From Wikipedia

The university moved in 1833 and has maintained its main campus in Greenwich Village surrounding Washington Square Park. Since then, the university has added an engineering school in Brooklyn's MetroTech Center and graduate schools throughout Manhattan. NYU is one of the largest private universities in the United States by enrollment, with a total of 61,890 enrolled students in 2023, and is one of the nation's most heavily applied–to universities with over 120,000 applications each year.
