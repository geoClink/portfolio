Planning the visit

  • Fly intoJohn Glenn Columbus International Airport (CMH)

    John Glenn Columbus International, east of downtown Columbus, is the main airport for the region.

  • Getting aroundWalking / CABS buses

    The free Campus Area Bus Service runs routes around campus, and COTA buses serve the rest of Columbus.

  • Colors and teams

    The school colors are scarlet and gray, and the teams are the Buckeyes.

    Thompson Library

    The Oval, Ohio State University, Columbus, OH 43210

    Boston firm Allen & Collens designed the main library in the Italian Renaissance Revival style, completed in 1912 on the Oval. A 2009 renovation restored its original east front.

    Ohio Stadium

    411 Woody Hayes Dr, Columbus, OH 43210

    Howard Dwight Smith designed this horseshoe-shaped stadium, opened in 1922 and listed on the National Register of Historic Places. Its north rotunda was modeled on the dome of the Pantheon in Rome.

    University Hall

    230 N Oval Mall, Columbus, OH 43210

    The first University Hall, a High Victorian Gothic design by Jacob Snyder from 1873, was torn down in 1971. The present building, completed in 1976, copies the original's architecture.

3 traditions to check off

Stamped! keeps these as a checklist, so you can tick each one off on the visit.

  • The Long Walk

    The Long Walk crosses the Oval toward University Hall; first-year students were once forbidden to use it. Walk its length across the heart of campus.

  • Buckeye Grove

    A buckeye tree is planted beside Ohio Stadium for each of Ohio State's first-team All-Americans, each with a plaque. Look for the names of famous players among the trees.

  • Victory Bell

    The Victory Bell in Ohio Stadium's southeast tower is rung after every Ohio State win. Listen for it after a home game.
