Small Business Platform Suite

Purpose-built platforms for small businesses — custom websites, admin dashboards, and iOS apps. Built at CH Studios LLC.

Small businesses — bakeries, salons, sports bars, wellness studios — are paying $30–150/month for platforms that weren't built for them. This suite is a purpose-built alternative: custom-designed, fully functional, and owned outright. No monthly platform fee.

THE
BAKERY
The Bakery Co.

Full business platform — customer website, admin dashboard, and Supabase backend. Owner manages menu, inventory, and orders from the dashboard; changes reflect instantly on the site and iOS apps. Stripe payments, JWT-authenticated CMS, transactional email and SMS, a production MCP server for Claude AI ordering, and WebMCP integration shipped the week Google announced it at I/O 2026.

Why No Framework

All four sites are hand-written HTML, CSS, and JavaScript — no React, no Next.js, no CMS. The decision is deliberate: a small business owner shouldn't need a developer on retainer to update their hours. The sites load fast, cost nothing to host beyond a domain, and can be handed off as static files. The technical complexity (fuzzy search, Stripe integration, MCP server) lives in the JavaScript — not in a framework dependency tree that goes stale in six months.
