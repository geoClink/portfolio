Small Business Platform Suite

A suite of purpose-built platforms for small businesses — each with a custom website, admin dashboard, and connected iOS apps. Built at CH Studios LLC.

Small businesses — bakeries, salons, sports bars, wellness studios — are paying $30–150/month for platforms that weren't built for them. The Small Business Platform Suite is a purpose-built alternative: custom-designed, fully functional, and owned outright. Each platform gives the owner a live website, a no-code admin dashboard to manage their content, and iOS apps that stay in sync with everything — no developer required after handoff. No monthly platform fee.
THE
BAKERY

The Bakery Co.

Live

The customer website, admin dashboard, and Supabase backend for a full business platform built at CH Studios. The owner logs into the admin dashboard, updates the menu or inventory, and the change reflects instantly on the website and across the iOS apps — no code, no redeploy. Stripe payments, JWT-authenticated admin CMS, transactional email and SMS, a production MCP server for Claude AI ordering, and WebMCP browser integration shipped the week Google announced it at I/O 2026. The iOS POS and customer app were built by CH Studios co-founder Jaiden Henley on the same database.

Why No Framework

All four sites are hand-written HTML, CSS, and JavaScript — no React, no Next.js, no CMS. The decision is deliberate: a small business owner shouldn't need a developer on retainer to update their hours. The sites load fast, cost nothing to host beyond a domain, and can be handed off as static files. The technical complexity (fuzzy search, Stripe integration, MCP server) lives in the JavaScript — not in a framework dependency tree that goes stale in six months.
