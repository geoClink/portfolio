The Athens of South America

Bogotá architecture

Bogotá sits at 2,600 meters above sea level, making it one of the world's highest capital cities — the altitude can catch visitors off guard with mild breathlessness on arrival. The city has undergone a remarkable urban transformation since the early 2000s, implementing a world-famous ciclovía: every Sunday, over 120km of roads are closed to traffic and opened exclusively to cyclists, joggers, and pedestrians — one of the largest car-free events in the world.

Before you go

  • Fly intoEl Dorado International Airport (BOG)

    El Dorado International Airport, named after the legendary city of gold sought by Spanish conquistadors, is Colombia's primary aviation hub and one of the busiest cargo airports in Latin America. Its Terminal 1, renovated in 2015, features a sweeping design that has won international architecture recognition. The airport sits at high altitude, which affects aircraft performance and contributes to the unique misty atmosphere of arrivals in Bogotá.

  • CurrencyColombian Peso ($)
  • LanguageSpanish

    Bogotá's Spanish is widely regarded as some of the clearest and most neutral-sounding in the Spanish-speaking world, making it popular for Spanish-language learners. The city's intellectual tradition — reflected in its UNESCO City of Book designation and the nickname Athens of South America — runs through everyday speech, which tends to be formal and eloquent. Colombian Spanish uses usted (formal you) even in casual and family settings, reflecting a culture of respectful address.

  • Getting aroundTransMilenio BRT

    Bogotá's TransMilenio is one of the world's largest and most successful Bus Rapid Transit (BRT) systems, with dedicated bus lanes and pre-boarding fare payment that allows it to move passengers at metro-like speeds across the city. It carries over 2 million passengers on a typical weekday. The city's permanent network of 550km of protected bike lanes — the largest in Latin America — makes cycling a mainstream way to get around.
