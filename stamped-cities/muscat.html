The Jewel of Arabia

Muscat architecture

Muscat is the capital of Oman, a sultanate known for its exceptional political stability, natural beauty, and warm hospitality in a region often defined by tension. The Sultan Qaboos Grand Mosque — completed in 2001 — can accommodate 20,000 worshippers and features the world's second-largest hand-woven Persian carpet, which took 600 women four years to complete. Oman's coastline stretches over 3,000km, offering everything from pristine beaches to dramatic fjords (locally called khors) in the Musandam Peninsula.

Before you go

  • Fly intoMuscat International Airport (MCT)

    Muscat International Airport underwent a complete transformation when its New Muscat International Airport (MCT) opened in 2018, replacing the old facility with a world-class terminal modeled on a sinuous desert landscape. The striking white terminal, inspired by Omani traditional architecture and the dunes of the Empty Quarter, significantly expanded the airport's capacity and passenger experience.

  • CurrencyOmani Rial (ر.ع.)
  • LanguageArabic

    Arabic is Oman's official language, with an Omani dialect that differs notably from Gulf Arabic in its unique vocabulary and some Persian loanwords absorbed through centuries of maritime trade. English is widely spoken in Muscat's business community, hotels, and tourist areas. The Omani culture is known for its exceptional hospitality — the traditional greeting involves offering frankincense smoke and dates to guests — reflecting a trading civilization with roots in the ancient Silk Road sea routes.

  • Getting aroundMwasalat Buses & Taxis

    Muscat's primary public transport is the Mwasalat bus network, which connects the city's spread-out districts and suburbs. A fleet of orange-and-white taxis and ride-hailing apps serve most visitor needs. Muscat is a spread-out city with no single compact center — its main areas of Muttrah, Old Muscat, Ruwi, and Qurum are connected by the coastal highway. Walking along the Muttrah Corniche at sunset is one of the city's most rewarding experiences.
