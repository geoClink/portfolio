The Mother City

Cape Town architecture

Cape Town is dominated by the majestic Table Mountain, which is estimated to be 600 million years old—older than the Andes and the Himalayas. The city is a biodiversity hotspot, home to the unique Fynbos vegetation and the famous penguin colony at Boulders Beach.

      Before you go

      • Fly intoCape Town Int'l (CPT)

        Consistently voted the best and cleanest airport in Africa, CPT is praised for its ease of navigation and friendly staff. It offers spectacular views of the Hottentots Holland Mountains during takeoff and has been recognized globally for its commitment to environmental sustainability.

      • CurrencySouth African Rand (R)
      • LanguageEnglish & Afrikaans

        The city features a melodic blend of English, Afrikaans, and the rhythmic clicks of Xhosa. English is the dominant language used in government, business, and by most visitors.

      • Getting aroundMyCiTi Bus

        The MyCiTi Bus system uses dedicated lanes and level-boarding stations that make it function much like a surface-level metro. Golden Arrow buses provide the traditional commuter service that has been operating in the city for over 150 years. The Metrorail Southern Line offers a scenic train journey along the coast towards the famous Kalk Bay.

      5 landmarks in Cape Town

      1. 01

        Zeitz MOCAA

        Silo District, S Arm Rd, V&A Waterfront, Cape Town, 8001, South Africa

        This architectural marvel is a breathtaking conversion of a historic 1920s grain silo complex. The interior was meticulously 'carved out' to create a cathedral-like atrium in the shape of a single grain of corn. It now houses the world's largest museum dedicated to contemporary art from Africa and its diaspora. The exterior is defined by its iconic 'pillow' glass windows that reflect the Atlantic Ocean and Table Mountain. This project successfully preserved the industrial heritage of the V&A Waterfront while creating a futuristic cultural landmark.

      2. 02

        Cape Town City Hall

        Darling St, Cape Town City Centre, Cape Town, 8001, South Africa

        This grand Edwardian building is a primary cultural landmark situated in the heart of the city center. It is most famous as the site where Nelson Mandela addressed thousands of people following his release from prison. The facade is constructed from honey-colored oolitic limestone imported specifically from Bath, England. The building features a massive clock tower modeled after London’s Big Ben, which houses a 39-bell carillon. Today, the hall serves as a prestigious venue for the Cape Town Philharmonic Orchestra and various civic exhibitions.

      3. 03

        Castle of Good Hope

        Darling St & Buitenkant St, Cape Town City Centre, Cape Town, 8001, South Africa

        As the oldest surviving colonial building in South Africa, this fortress represents the earliest European architecture in the region. The pentagonal star-shaped layout was designed as a state-of-the-art defense system by the Dutch East India Company. It features a distinctive yellow lime wash on its walls, a color chosen to reduce the glare of the intense African sun. Inside, the complex contains the William Fehr Collection, showcasing historical paintings and antique Cape furniture. The site remains a powerful symbol of the military and political history that shaped the Western Cape.

      4. 04

        Portside Tower

        4 Bree St, Cape Town City Centre, Cape Town, 8001, South Africa

        Portside Tower stands as the tallest skyscraper in Cape Town, dominating the central business district skyline. It is a pioneer of sustainable design, being the first tall building in the country to earn a 5-Star Green Star rating. The high-performance glass facade is engineered to maximize natural daylight while preventing excessive solar heat gain. Its sleek, modern silhouette serves as the regional headquarters for major financial institutions and global law firms. The building is equipped with advanced energy-saving systems, including LED lighting and extensive bicycle storage for commuters.

      5. 05

        Groot Constantia Manor House

        Groot Constantia Rd, Constantia, Cape Town, 7806, South Africa

        This manor house is regarded as the finest example of Cape Dutch architecture in South Africa. Its design is characterized by ornate central gables, whitewashed walls, and a traditional dark thatched roof. The estate was founded in 1685, making it the oldest wine-producing farm in the southern hemisphere. The building was meticulously restored after a fire in 1925 to preserve its 18th-century appearance and cultural integrity. Today, it functions as a museum where visitors can explore the intersection of European style and local craftsmanship.
