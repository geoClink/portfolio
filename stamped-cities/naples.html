City of the Sun

Naples architecture

Naples is the legendary birthplace of pizza, where the Margherita style was first created in 1889 to honor the visiting Queen of Italy. The city sits atop a complex network of ancient tunnels and catacombs, while the looming presence of Mount Vesuvius dominates its scenic bay.

Before you go

  • Fly intoNaples Int'l (NAP)

    Naples International is unique because its runways offer pilots and passengers direct, dramatic views of the active volcano, Mount Vesuvius. Despite its age, the airport has been modernized into a boutique travel hub that perfectly captures the vibrant and chaotic charm of the city it serves.

  • CurrencyEuro (€)
  • LanguageItalian

    While Italian is the official language, the local Neapolitan language is so distinct that it is recognized by UNESCO as its own separate entity. It is an expressive and rhythmic way of speaking that is often accompanied by the city's famous theatrical hand gestures.

  • Getting aroundMetro Art Stations

    The Naples Metro is world-famous for its 'Art Stations,' with the Toledo station often cited as Europe's most beautiful. The Circumvesuviana is a historic narrow-gauge railway that connects the city to Pompeii and Sorrento. Four different funicular lines help residents navigate the city's extremely steep and hilly terrain.

13 landmarks in Naples

  1. 01

    Castel dell'Ovo (Egg Castle)

    Via Eldorado, 3, 80132 Napoli NA, Italy

    The oldest standing fortification in Naples was constructed on the site of a former Roman villa. Its unique name stems from a legend involving the poet Virgil, who reportedly buried a magical egg beneath its foundations. This yellow tuff stone fortress sits on the small island of Megaride, which is connected to the city by a scenic causeway. It has served as a gritty, romantic sentinel of the Neapolitan harbor for nearly a thousand years. Today, the castle provides a stunning backdrop for photography and serves as a premier venue for cultural exhibitions.

  2. 02

    Galleria Umberto I

    Via San Carlo, 15, 80132 Napoli NA, Italy

    This masterpiece of 19th-century urban design is one of the most famous public shopping galleries in Italy. It is celebrated for its massive iron and glass dome that reaches a height of 57 meters. The sprawling marble mosaic floor represents the various signs of the zodiac in beautiful detail. It was built during the 'Risanamento' period to revitalize the city's infrastructure and hygiene. Its Belle Époque elegance offers a sophisticated contrast to the chaotic energy of the neighboring Spanish Quarter.

  3. 03

    Duomo di Napoli (Naples Cathedral)

    Via Duomo, 147, 80138 Napoli NA, Italy

    The city's principal place of worship features a complex architectural blend of Gothic, Renaissance, and Baroque styles. It is most famous for housing the Royal Chapel of the Treasure of San Gennaro, the city's patron saint. The cathedral was constructed atop the foundations of two earlier Paleo-Christian churches. Visitors can still explore the ancient archaeological ruins that lie beneath the modern street level. It remains the spiritual heart of Naples and a site of major religious significance for the local population.

  4. 04

    Teatro di San Carlo

    Via Vittorio Emanuele III, 80132 Napoli NA, Italy

    This historic venue is the oldest continuously active opera house in the entire world. Its stunning blue-and-gold auditorium is globally renowned for its exceptional acoustics and massive royal box. The building’s exterior facade is a prime example of Neoclassical sobriety and architectural restraint. This simple exterior conceals an incredibly lavish and ornate tiered seating area within. It continues to host major international opera and ballet performances throughout the year.

  5. 05

    Certosa di San Martino

    Largo S. Martino, 5, 80129 Napoli NA, Italy

    Perched high on the Vomero hill, this former monastery offers arguably the best panoramic view of the Bay of Naples. It stands as one of the most successful and complete examples of Neapolitan Baroque architecture. The complex includes a breathtaking marble cloister designed by the artist Cosimo Fanzago. Within its walls, a museum houses an extensive collection of famous Neapolitan Nativity scenes known as 'Presepe.' The structures are crafted from a beautiful combination of local white marble and grey volcanic stone.

  6. 06

    Toledo Metro Station

    Via Toledo, 80134 Napoli NA, Italy

    This facility is frequently cited as the most beautiful subway station in Europe by international design experts. It serves as a flagship project for the city's ongoing 'Art Stations' urban renewal initiative. The design centers on the 'Crater de Luz,' a massive light well that filters sunlight deep into the earth. Millions of blue and white Bisazza mosaics cover the walls to create an immersive aquatic atmosphere. Commuters are treated to a surreal experience that feels like walking through a contemporary art installation.

  7. 07

    Palazzo Reale (Royal Palace of Naples)

    Piazza del Plebiscito, 1, 80132 Napoli NA, Italy

    This grand palace was one of the four primary residences used by the Bourbon Kings of Naples. It dominates the eastern side of the Piazza del Plebiscito, the city’s largest public square. The facade is famously adorned with eight statues representing the most significant dynasties of the kingdom. Inside, the massive 'Scalone d'Onore' staircase is a triumph of Neoclassical white marble design. The palace now houses the opulent Royal Apartments and the historic collection of the National Library.

  8. 08

    Castel Nuovo (Maschio Angioino)

    Via Vittorio Emanuele III, 80133 Napoli NA, Italy

    This imposing medieval castle is distinguished by its five massive round towers made of dark volcanic stone. It features a striking white marble Triumphal Arch that was built to celebrate the entry of Alfonso of Aragon. The castle served as a royal residence for several centuries under various Mediterranean ruling dynasties. Its strategic location near the harbor underscores the historical maritime power of the Neapolitan kingdom. Today, it remains an enduring symbol of the city and houses the Civic Museum and local government offices.

  9. 09

    Museo Archeologico Nazionale di Napoli (MANN)

    Piazza Museo, 19, 80135 Napoli NA, Italy

    Located within a former 16th-century cavalry barracks, this institution is one of the world's premier archaeological museums. It houses the celebrated Farnese collection, which includes colossal sculptures from the Greco-Roman era. The museum is also the primary repository for artifacts recovered from the ruins of Pompeii and Herculaneum. The architectural design is noted for its deep Pompeian-red walls and expansive vaulted galleries. These massive halls were specifically designed to support the incredible weight of ancient stone masterpieces.

  10. 10

    Basilica di Santa Chiara

    Via Benedetto Croce, 18, 80134 Napoli NA, Italy

    This religious complex is notable for its stark Provencal-Gothic architecture, which is rare in this part of Italy. The primary highlight of the site is the Cloister of the Clarisses with its ornate outdoor walkways. It features sixty-six octagonal pillars adorned with hand-painted 'Majolica' tiles depicting colorful scenes of rural life. The complex acts as a peaceful urban oasis hidden behind high, protective volcanic stone walls. It serves as both a monastery and a museum, preserving centuries of Neapolitan religious heritage.

  11. 11

    Cappella Sansevero

    Via Francesco de Sanctis, 19, 80134 Napoli NA, Italy

    This small chapel is widely considered a jewel of global artistic heritage due to its dense collection of Baroque art. It is most famous for the 'Veiled Christ,' a sculpture noted for the miraculous realism of its marble veil. The building was designed as an alchemical temple by the eccentric Prince of Sansevero. Its crypt contains the mysterious 'anatomical machines,' which are two skeletons with detailed ironwork venous systems. Every corner of the masonry is filled with complex symbolic meanings and masterful craftsmanship.

  12. 12

    Stazione Marittima di Napoli

    Molo Angioino, 80133 Napoli NA, Italy

    This massive structure is a prominent example of Rationalist architecture from the Fascist era in Italy. Located in the center of the active port, it features two long symmetrical wings and a central tower. The building’s design uses local stone to create a stripped-down, modern interpretation of classical forms. It was originally built as a grand gateway to the city for passengers arriving on ocean liners. Today, it has been modernized to serve as a busy cruise terminal and an urban shopping center.

  13. 13

    Apple Via Toledo

    Via Toledo, 177/178, 80134 Napoli NA, Italy

    This modern retail space is housed in a meticulously restored historic palazzo on one of the city's most famous streets. The restoration preserves original architectural details like grand vaulted ceilings and intricate stonework. These historic elements are contrasted with minimalist glass fixtures and modern wooden tables. It functions as a contemporary 'piazza' where the local community can gather for technology workshops. The building successfully blends the historic charm of old Naples with cutting-edge design and technology.
