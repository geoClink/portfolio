City guides / Africa / Kenya

Green City in the Sun

Nairobi architecture

Nairobi is the only city in the world that features a full-scale National Park within its borders, where lions and rhinos roam against a backdrop of skyscrapers. It has also emerged as a leading tech hub in Africa, often referred to as the 'Silicon Savannah' for its thriving mobile money innovations.

Before you go

  • Fly intoJomo Kenyatta (NBO)

    Serving as the primary gateway for East African safaris, the airport is a bustling hub for travelers heading to the Maasai Mara and beyond. It has recently undergone significant renovations to modernize its terminals and improve the transit experience for its growing number of international visitors.

  • CurrencyKenyan Shilling (KSh)
  • LanguageSwahili & English

    English and Swahili are both official languages, though you will often hear 'Sheng,' a cool blend of the two used by locals. English is the primary language for instruction and business.

  • Getting aroundMatatus

    Matatus are vibrant, private minibuses famous for their elaborate graffiti, custom lighting, and loud sound systems. The standard-gauge railway (SGR) provides a modern, high-speed link between Nairobi and the coastal city of Mombasa. Commuter trains and Boda-Bodas (motorcycle taxis) are essential for navigating the city's heavy traffic.

8 landmarks in Nairobi

  1. 01

    Britam Tower

    Hospital Rd, Upper Hill, Nairobi 00200, Kenya

    This prismatic skyscraper is a 32-story marvel that elegantly tapers to a distinct architectural point. Its unique design incorporates advanced ceramic fins that provide essential solar shading and wind resistance for the glass facade. Standing at 200 meters, it remains one of the tallest architectural achievements on the African continent today. The tower serves as a defining feature of the rapidly evolving Upper Hill financial district skyline. Its energy-efficient design has earned it international recognition as a leader in sustainable high-rise development across East Africa.

  2. 02

    Kenyatta International Convention Centre

    Harambee Ave, City Square, Nairobi 00100, Kenya

    This structure stands as Nairobi's most iconic brutalist landmark and a powerful symbol of Kenyan national pride. The tower's terracotta-colored cylinder and amphitheater were creatively inspired by the aesthetics of traditional African huts. Its rooftop features a 360-degree helipad that offers unparalleled views of the city and the nearby Nairobi National Park. For decades, it served as the tallest building in the region and a primary hub for international diplomacy. It continues to function as a premier venue for global conferences and significant cultural events in the heart of the capital.

  3. 03

    iStore Westgate (Apple Premium)

    Westgate Shopping Mall, Mwanzi Rd, Westlands, Nairobi 00606, Kenya

    Located within the upscale Westgate Mall, this site serves as the flagship Apple Premium Partner for the East African region. It acts as the primary technological hub for the growing 'Silicon Savannah' community of local developers and designers. The interior design mirrors the clean, minimalist aesthetic found in Apple retail locations across the globe. Large glass panes and bright open spaces create a sanctuary for local tech enthusiasts and creative professionals. It represents the successful intersection of global retail standards and Nairobi's thriving modern consumer culture in the Westlands area.

  4. 04

    GTC Office Tower

    Waiyaki Way, Westlands, Nairobi 00800, Kenya

    As of 2026, this sleek skyscraper stands as the tallest dedicated office tower in the entire East African region. The exterior glass facade utilizes advanced Low-E technology to efficiently regulate internal temperatures against the equatorial sun. It features a spectacular observation deck that provides visitors with a bird's-eye view of the city's green spaces and urban sprawl. The building is a central component of the Global Trade Centre, attracting numerous Fortune 500 companies to the area. Its rooftop helipad and modern silhouette have made it the primary focal point of the Westlands commercial skyline.

  5. 05

    All Saints' Cathedral

    Kenyatta Ave, Nairobi 00100, Kenya

    This cathedral is a magnificent example of British Colonial-era Gothic architecture located in the heart of Nairobi. Construction took place over several decades, resulting in a rich tapestry of local stone and intricate imported craftsmanship. The interior is beautifully illuminated by stunning stained-glass windows that depict various religious and historical themes pertinent to Kenya. It serves as the official seat of the Anglican Church in Kenya and remains a major spiritual pillar for the community. Even as modern skyscrapers rise around it, the cathedral remains a peaceful sanctuary of historical preservation and architectural integrity.

  6. 06

    UAP Old Mutual Tower

    Upper Hill Rd, Nairobi 00200, Kenya

    Taking clear inspiration from the Empire State Building, this tower was designed to be a prominent and permanent landmark in Upper Hill. It is widely recognized for its dichromatic facade and the translucent glass pyramid that crowns its peak. At night, the glass pyramid glows with colorful LED lights to create a beacon across the Nairobi skyline. The building sits on one of the highest geographical points in the city, which significantly enhances its visual impact. It provides high-end office space for major financial institutions and many international corporate firms operating in the region.

  7. 07

    Nairobi National Museum

    Kipande Rd, Museum Hill, Nairobi 00100, Kenya

    The Nairobi National Museum is a majestic architectural blend of colonial heritage and modern expansion set within lush botanical gardens. Originally established in 1910, the current facility underwent a massive modernization in 2005 to create a world-class cultural hub. Its stone facades and broad entrances lead to galleries that house some of the most important early human fossils in existence. The building serves as a multidisciplinary center for Kenya's history, nature, culture, and contemporary East African art. It is famously guarded by a life-size bronze statue of Ahmed, the legendary elephant who became a national symbol of wildlife protection.

  8. 08

    CBK Pension Tower

    Harambee Ave, City Square, Nairobi 00100, Kenya

    The CBK Pension Tower is a striking architectural marvel consisting of two sky-blue glass towers that curve away from each other. Designed by local Kenyan architects, the building's unique silhouette is meant to symbolize two senior citizens leaning back to back in retirement. This ultra-modern skyscraper is one of the greenest in the city, featuring integrated solar panels that power the facility during the day. It stands as a testament to the Central Bank of Kenya's modernization and its commitment to sustainable urban development in the Central Business District. The tower has quickly become a favorite for photographers due to its reflective glass and its status as a modern structure in downtown Nairobi.
