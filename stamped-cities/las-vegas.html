Sin City

Las Vegas architecture

The Las Vegas Strip is so bright that it is the most luminous place on Earth when viewed from outer space. Beyond the casinos, the city is surrounded by the stunning Mojave Desert and is just a short drive from the engineering marvel of the Hoover Dam.

Before you go

  • Fly intoHarry Reid Int'l (LAS)

    Harry Reid International is one of the very few airports in the world that features slot machines throughout the terminals, allowing visitors to gamble right at their gates. It serves as a 24-hour gateway to the world's entertainment capital, handling millions of tourists and convention-goers every year.

  • CurrencyUS Dollar ($)
  • LanguageEnglish

    The city employs a fast-paced, 24/7 vernacular largely driven by the high-energy tourism and hospitality industries. Because of the global influx of visitors, the local English is extremely accessible, though it is often peppered with the specific jargon of the gaming and entertainment worlds.

  • Getting aroundMonorail & Deuce

    The Las Vegas Monorail is a 100% electric system that produces zero emissions while connecting major resorts. The Deuce is a famous fleet of gold double-decker buses that run 24/7 along the Las Vegas Strip. Several independent trams also offer free, short-distance connections between specific hotel clusters.

11 landmarks in Las Vegas

  1. 01

    Luxor Las Vegas

    3900 S Las Vegas Blvd, Las Vegas, NV 89119

    This iconic thirty-story black glass pyramid remains one of the most recognizable structures on the Las Vegas Strip. It features the Luxor Sky Beam, which is officially the strongest concentrated beam of light in the entire world. The interior contains the world's largest atrium by volume, creating a vast and awe-inspiring indoor space for guests. Its unique design was inspired by the Great Pyramid of Giza and incorporates modern Egyptian Revival aesthetic elements throughout. Instead of traditional vertical elevators, the building uses inclinators that travel at a sharp thirty-nine-degree angle along the corners.

  2. 02

    The STRAT Hotel & Tower

    2000 S Las Vegas Blvd, Las Vegas, NV 89104

    This landmark is the tallest freestanding observation tower in the United States, dominating the northern end of the Strip. Its unique silhouette is topped by a multi-story pod that houses a revolving restaurant and several high-altitude lounges. Thrill-seekers can experience extreme rides located at the very top of the tower, hundreds of meters above the ground. The tower's sturdy legs are constructed from reinforced concrete designed to withstand intense desert winds and seismic activity. It serves as a primary navigation point for both tourists and locals due to its immense height and visibility.

  3. 03

    Bellagio Las Vegas

    3600 S Las Vegas Blvd, Las Vegas, NV 89109

    This elegant resort was modeled after the picturesque Lake Como resort of Bellagio located in northern Italy. The building is world-famous for its massive eight-acre lake that hosts synchronized dancing water fountain performances. It effectively set a new standard for luxury on the Strip when it opened in the late 1990s. Inside, the property features a stunning Conservatory and Botanical Garden that changes its floral displays five times a year. The Mediterranean-inspired architecture and high-end amenities continue to attract millions of visitors seeking a sophisticated Vegas experience.

  4. 04

    Sphere at The Venetian Resort

    255 Sands Ave, Las Vegas, NV 89169

    This revolutionary venue is currently the world's largest spherical structure and a major feat of modern engineering. The exterior 'Exosphere' consists of a five hundred eighty thousand square foot fully programmable LED display system. Inside the dome, a 16K resolution wrap-around LED screen provides an immersive visual experience unlike any other theater. The auditorium features specialized haptic seats that vibrate to match the intensity of the audio and on-screen action. It has quickly become a global icon for the next generation of live music and immersive cinematic storytelling.

  5. 05

    Caesars Palace

    3570 S Las Vegas Blvd, Las Vegas, NV 89109

    This historic landmark originally defined the era of large-scale themed resorts in the city of Las Vegas. It was designed to provide guests with the opulent feeling of being in the heart of the Roman Empire. The sprawling campus features classic Corinthian columns, marble statues, and the famous Colosseum entertainment theater. Over many decades, the property has undergone numerous expansions to include a massive collection of six distinct hotel towers. It remains a primary destination for high-stakes gaming and world-class culinary experiences from celebrity chefs.

  6. 06

    Lou Ruvo Center for Brain Health

    888 W Bonneville Ave, Las Vegas, NV 89106

    This building stands as a stunning and complex example of deconstructivist architecture in the downtown area. One side of the structure functions as a highly efficient medical center dedicated to patient care and research. In contrast, the other side is a gleaming stainless steel event center designed to host high-profile fundraisers. The facade features one hundred ninety-nine unique windows, none of which share the same exact size or shape. Frank Gehry's design successfully merges the sterile needs of medicine with the artistic expression of a modern landmark.

  7. 07

    Las Vegas City Hall

    495 S Main St, Las Vegas, NV 89101

    This LEED Gold-certified civic building serves as a model for sustainable urban architecture in a harsh desert climate. The glass facade is integrated with solar fins that generate renewable electricity while providing essential cooling shade. Its central interior atrium was specifically designed to maximize the use of natural light throughout the workday. The exterior glass panels are arranged to create a shimmering mirage effect that reflects the surrounding desert environment. It represents a significant shift toward modern and environmentally conscious government facilities in the downtown district.

  8. 08

    Circus Circus Adventuredome

    2880 S Las Vegas Blvd, Las Vegas, NV 89109

    This massive five-acre indoor theme park is entirely enclosed within a distinctive pink-tinted glass dome. When it was first constructed, the facility was recognized as one of the largest space-frame structures in the world. The dome's vibrant color was achieved through a specialized glass coating designed to block intense Nevada solar heat. It allows families to enjoy roller coasters and various attractions in a climate-controlled environment year-round. The structure remains a core part of the Circus Circus resort's identity and its appeal to traveling families.

  9. 09

    New York-New York Hotel

    3790 S Las Vegas Blvd, Las Vegas, NV 89109

    This resort serves as an architectural homage to the iconic skyline of New York City through several scaled replicas. The property features famous landmarks such as the Empire State Building, the Chrysler Building, and the Statue of Liberty. A high-speed roller coaster known as the Big Apple Coaster weaves directly around the exterior of the hotel towers. This integration of a thrill ride into a skyscraper's structure was a significant engineering feat at the time. It offers an energetic and themed experience that captures the bustling spirit of Manhattan in the middle of the desert.

  10. 10

    Encore at Wynn Las Vegas

    3131 S Las Vegas Blvd, Las Vegas, NV 89109

    This elegant curved bronze-glass tower was designed to mirror and complement its neighboring sister property, Wynn Las Vegas. It is widely celebrated for its Encore Beach Club, which helped popularize the integrated indoor-outdoor club concept. The architectural design prioritizes organic, sweeping curves rather than the sharp geometric angles found in older hotels. Luxurious interior spaces are filled with natural light and high-end finishes that maintain a sense of boutique exclusivity. It represents the pinnacle of modern resort design with a focus on seamless flow and sophisticated aesthetics.

  11. 11

    Apple The Forum Shops

    3500 S Las Vegas Blvd, Las Vegas, NV 89109

    This unique retail store is seamlessly integrated into the grand Roman-style architecture of the Forum Shops at Caesars Palace. The entrance features a striking circular stone entryway that invites customers into a wide and open gallery space. Inside, the design employs Apple's signature clean lines and minimalist furniture to create a modern retail atmosphere. This minimalist aesthetic provides a sharp and interesting contrast to the surrounding ornate statues and sky-painted ceilings. It stands as a premier example of how a modern brand can adapt its identity within a highly themed environment.
