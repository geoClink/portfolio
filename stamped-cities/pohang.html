City guides / Asia / South Korea

City of Light and Fire

Pohang architecture

Pohang is the industrial powerhouse of Korea, home to POSCO, one of the world's largest steel producers, which has fueled the nation's rapid economic growth. It is also famous for the 'Homigot Sunrise Square,' where a giant bronze hand sculpture rises from the sea to symbolize the dawn of the new millennium.

Before you go

  • Fly intoPohang Gyeongju (KPO)

    The airport recently changed its name to Pohang Gyeongju to better serve as a high-tech gateway to the historic neighboring region of Gyeongju, the ancient capital of the Silla Kingdom. It is a compact and efficient facility that allows travelers to transition quickly from the industrial city to the serene mountains and sea.

  • CurrencySouth Korean Won (₩)
  • LanguageKorean

    Locals in Pohang speak with the strong, tonal 'Gyeongsang' dialect, which can sound quite distinct from the standard Seoul accent. While English is less common than in the capital, the industrial nature of the city means business signs are often bilingual.

  • Getting aroundPohang Bus

    The Pohang bus system uses a smart information network that is accurate to the second for all arrivals. KTX high-speed trains connect Pohang directly to Seoul in under three hours from the modern Pohang Station. Shared 'Biseu' on-demand transit is being tested to provide better coverage for the city's outlying industrial areas.

11 landmarks in Pohang

  1. 01

    Space Walk

    30 Hwanhogongwon-gil, Buk-gu, Pohang-si, Gyeongsangbuk-do

    This massive looping steel structure is Korea's largest experiential sculpture, allowing visitors to walk directly on its tracks. It was designed to mimic the feeling of walking through the clouds or floating in outer space. The structure's elegant curves are made from high-strength steel produced locally by POSCO. At night, integrated LED lighting transforms the walkway into a glowing landmark visible from across the bay. It offers a panoramic 360-degree view of the city, the sea, and the surrounding Hwanho Park.

  2. 02

    Yeongildae Maritime Pavilion

    95 Haean-ro, Buk-gu, Pohang-si, Gyeongsangbuk-do

    This traditional two-story pavilion is uniquely constructed on pillars extending directly into the East Sea. It serves as a beautiful example of modern Hanok architecture blending with a maritime environment. Visitors cross the Yeongilgyu Bridge to reach the pavilion, which provides a cooling breeze even in peak summer. The structure is particularly famous for its sunrise views and its vibrant illumination after sunset. It has become the definitive symbol of Yeongildae Beach, the largest beach on Korea's eastern coast.

  3. 03

    POSCO Global Center

    6261 Donghaean-ro, Nam-gu, Pohang-si, Gyeongsangbuk-do

    This striking facility serves as the headquarters and administrative heart for one of the world's largest steel producers. The building's design emphasizes transparency and strength, utilizing advanced steel-glass curtain wall systems. It houses a public gallery and a museum that chronicles the industrial transformation of South Korea. The surrounding landscape is meticulously manicured to integrate the massive industrial scale with green public spaces. It stands as a testament to the city's identity as a global leader in the steel industry.

  4. 04

    Guryongpo Japanese Houses Street

    153-1 Guryongpo-gil, Guryongpo-eup, Nam-gu, Pohang-si, Gyeongsangbuk-do

    This historic district contains a well-preserved row of wooden houses dating back to the Japanese colonial occupation. The street was originally established by Japanese fishermen who settled in the area during the early 20th century. Today, it serves as a living museum and cultural space that reflects a complex period of Korean history. Many of the buildings have been converted into tea houses, small museums, and specialty craft shops. The area gained massive popularity recently as the primary filming location for several famous Korean television dramas.

  5. 05

    Pohang Museum of Steel Art (POMA)

    10 Hwanhogongwon-gil, Buk-gu, Pohang-si, Gyeongsangbuk-do

    As the world's first museum dedicated specifically to steel-based art, POMA is a cornerstone of the city's cultural life. The building's minimalist modern design features large floor-to-ceiling windows that allow natural light to play off the metallic sculptures. Its outdoor sculpture park seamlessly blends contemporary industrial art with the lush greenery of Hwanho Park. The museum regularly hosts international exhibitions that explore the intersection of technology, industry, and fine art. Educational programs offered here allow visitors to try their hand at metalworking and craft.

  6. 06

    Homigot Lighthouse

    99 Homigot-gil, Homigot-myeon, Nam-gu, Pohang-si, Gyeongsangbuk-do

    Located at the 'tiger's tail' of the Korean peninsula, this lighthouse has guided ships since the early 20th century. It was constructed using unique brickwork techniques in a Renaissance Revival style that remains rare in Korea. The structure is octagonal and narrows toward the top, standing as a masterpiece of functional historical architecture. Despite being over a century old, the lighthouse is still operational and serves as a major national cultural property. The surrounding plaza is home to the famous 'Hand of Sangsaeng' sculpture and the National Lighthouse Museum.

  7. 07

    POSTECH Chanel Hall

    77 Cheongam-ro, Nam-gu, Pohang-si, Gyeongsangbuk-do

    This iconic auditorium serves as the primary ceremonial space for one of the world's leading science and technology universities. Its Late Modernist design features a dramatic glass facade and a cavernous, acoustically optimized interior. The building is named in honor of the major donor who supported its construction to foster cultural exchange on campus. It stands at the center of the university's grid-based master plan, acting as a bridge between academic and social life. The hall is frequently used for international symposiums, classical concerts, and university-wide celebrations.

  8. 08

    Oeosa Temple

    1 Oeo-ro, Ocheon-eup, Nam-gu, Pohang-si, Gyeongsangbuk-do

    Originally founded during the Silla Dynasty, this temple is legendary for its association with the great monk Wonhyo. The current structures were meticulously restored to preserve the traditional Dancheong decorative painting style. It is uniquely situated between a steep mountain cliff and the tranquil waters of Oeo Lake. A long suspension bridge leads visitors across the water to the temple entrance, providing a meditative approach. The temple's quiet, secluded atmosphere makes it a preferred destination for those seeking spiritual reflection and natural beauty.

  9. 09

    Park 1538

    6262 Donghaean-ro, Nam-gu, Pohang-si, Gyeongsangbuk-do

    Park 1538 is a cutting-edge cultural complex that showcases the past, present, and future of steel innovation. The name refers to the melting point of pure iron, symbolizing the heat and energy of the steelmaking process. Its architecture features flowing, parametric lines and extensive use of stainless steel surfaces that reflect the sky. The facility includes a high-tech history museum, an art gallery, and a circular sky bridge that connects the different zones. It serves as a public-facing park that integrates industrial education with sophisticated urban design.

  10. 10

    Pohang Canal Pavilion

    Huirak-ro, Nam-gu, Pohang-si, Gyeongsangbuk-do

    This modern pavilion was built to celebrate the successful restoration of the historic waterway connecting the inner city to the harbor. The building's sleek, wave-like roofline is meant to evoke the movement of water and the city's maritime heritage. It functions as both an observation deck and the main terminal for the popular Pohang Canal cruise ships. From the top floors, visitors can enjoy clear views of the busy shipping lanes and the glowing POSCO steelworks across the water. It has revitalized the surrounding Dongbin-naehang area into a popular pedestrian and tourist destination.

  11. 11

    Apple Developer Academy (POSTECH C5)

    77 Cheongam-ro, Nam-gu, Pohang-si, Gyeongsangbuk-do (POSTECH C5)

    Housed within the C5 research building, this academy is the first of its kind in South Korea. The interior was completely redesigned to reflect Apple's signature aesthetic of openness and collaboration. It features flexible 'Big Table' workspaces and high-tech labs designed to encourage cross-disciplinary innovation among students. The space serves as an incubator for tech talent, blending Silicon Valley culture with the academic rigor of POSTECH. Its minimalist design utilizes glass, aluminum, and warm wood to create a focused yet creative environment for future developers.
