Eero Saarinen

Stamped! has 10 buildings by Eero Saarinen, in Columbus, Indiana, Massachusetts Institute of Technology, Milwaukee and 3 more. They date from 1955 (Irwin Conference Center) to 2001 (Milwaukee Art Museum).
