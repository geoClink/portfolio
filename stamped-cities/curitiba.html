Curitiba architecture

Brazil is a continental giant, occupying nearly half of South America and serving as the world's largest producer of coffee for over 150 years. It is a nation defined by its vibrant cultural melting pot, ranging from the Amazon rainforest to the world-famous Carnival celebrations in Rio.

Before you go

  • Fly intoGuarulhos Int'l (GRU)

    As the primary international gateway to Brazil, Guarulhos is the busiest airport in South America by passenger volume. It is a massive complex that was significantly modernized for the World Cup and Olympics, featuring a dedicated terminal for long-haul international flights with extensive luxury amenities.

  • CurrencyBrazilian Real (R$)
  • LanguagePortuguese

    Portuguese is the sole official language and is spoken with a musical, open accent that differs significantly from the European version. Most locals appreciate any effort to speak 'Português' and are very patient with learners.

  • Getting aroundCuritiba BRT & Metrô

    Curitiba was the global pioneer of the 'Bus as a Train' concept, using iconic glass tube stations for level boarding. São Paulo's Metrô is one of the cleanest and most efficient in the world, serving millions of passengers across six lines. Rio de Janeiro utilizes the 'Bondinho' tram in Santa Teresa and a modern VLT light rail system in the revitalized port area.

  1. 01

    Botanical Garden of Curitiba

    Rua Engenheiro Ostoja Roguski, s/n, Jardim Botânico, Curitiba, PR, 80210-390, Brazil

    The Botanical Garden is the most famous postcard image of Curitiba and serves as a major center for environmental research. Its iconic Art Nouveau greenhouse was inspired by the 19th-century Crystal Palace in London and features a delicate white metal structure. The greenhouse is divided into three distinct domes that house diverse specimens of tropical and subtropical flora. Surrounding the glass house are extensive French-style formal gardens with geometric flowerbeds and symmetrical fountains. It is a vital green space for the city and remains a primary destination for both international tourists and local residents seeking leisure.

  2. 02

    Oscar Niemeyer Museum (MON)

    Rua Marechal Hermes, 999, Centro Cívico, Curitiba, PR, 80530-230, Brazil

    Widely known as 'The Eye' due to its bold sculptural shape, this museum is one of the largest and most significant art institutions in Latin America. The 30-meter-high tower consists of a massive glass and concrete eye perched atop a bright yellow pedestal. Designed by the legendary Oscar Niemeyer, the building reflects his signature style of sweeping curves and bold geometric volumes. The complex features vast underground galleries and an expansive park known as 'Parcão' where locals gather with their pets. It hosts a world-class collection of contemporary art, design, and architecture within its unconventional and striking halls.

  3. 03

    Wire Opera House (Ópera de Arame)

    Rua João Gava, 970, Abranches, Curitiba, PR, 82130-010, Brazil

    Constructed in just 75 days, the Wire Opera House is a high-tech marvel built entirely from steel tubes and transparent polycarbonate. The theater is situated in a former stone quarry, creating a dramatic interplay between industrial architecture and raw nature. Access to the building is granted via a bridge over a man-made lake, which is fed by a cascading waterfall from the quarry walls. The transparent walls allow the surrounding forest to become a visual part of every performance held inside. It is part of the Parque das Pedreiras complex and remains one of the city's most emblematic cultural venues.

  4. 04

    University of the Environment (Unilivre)

    Rua Victor Benato, 210, Pilarzinho, Curitiba, PR, 82120-110, Brazil

    The Free University of the Environment is a unique educational center focused on ecological awareness and sustainable practices. Its striking spiral structure was built primarily from recycled wooden utility poles, symbolizing the institution's commitment to the planet. The ramp winds upward through the tree canopy, leading visitors to an observation deck overlooking a flooded quarry lake. This architectural choice minimizes the impact on the surrounding Zaninelli Woods while providing a meditative path for students. It stands as a global example of how recycled materials can be used to create beautiful and functional institutional spaces.

  5. 05

    Panoramic Tower (Torre das Mercês)

    Rua Lycio Grein Castro Vellozo, 191, Mercês, Curitiba, PR, 80710-650, Brazil

    Standing at 109 meters, the Panoramic Tower is a functional telecommunications facility that doubles as Curitiba's highest observation point. The tower features a circular glass-walled deck that offers an unobstructed 360-degree view of the city and the distant Serra do Mar mountains. Inside the observation level, a large floor map helps visitors identify the different neighborhoods and landmarks spread out below. The tower also houses the Museum of Telephones, which documents the history of communication in the state of Paraná. It is an essential stop for photographers and travelers looking to understand the unique urban grid of the city.

  6. 06

    Paço da Liberdade

    Praça Generoso Marques, s/n, Centro, Curitiba, PR, 80020-230, Brazil

    The Paço da Liberdade is an architectural jewel in Curitiba's historic center and formerly served as the city's first seat of government. It is the only building in the city listed as a national heritage site by IPHAN due to its significant cultural value. The Eclectic design features intricate Art Nouveau details, including ornate plasterwork and original stained glass. After a careful restoration, it now functions as a vibrant cultural center managed by SESC, hosting a library and cinema. The building stands in the middle of a lively square, serving as a reminder of Curitiba's early 20th-century urban prosperity.

  7. 07

    Ukrainian Memorial

    Rua Mbaiguá, s/n, Parque Tingui, São João, Curitiba, PR, 82130-100, Brazil

    Located within Tingui Park, this memorial honors the massive wave of Ukrainian immigrants who helped build the state of Paraná. The central structure is a faithful replica of the wooden Church of Saint Michael the Archangel found in the Ukrainian countryside. It was constructed using traditional methods, featuring wooden shingles and the characteristic rounded domes of Eastern Orthodox architecture. Inside, the space serves as a museum displaying 'pêssankas'—intricately hand-painted eggs—and other traditional folk crafts. The peaceful surroundings include a statue of the poet Taras Shevchenko, making it a place of deep cultural pride.

  8. 08

    Estação Tubo (Tube Station)

    Praça Tiradentes, s/n, Centro, Curitiba, PR, 80020-100, Brazil

    The Tube Stations are the internationally recognized symbol of Curitiba's pioneering Bus Rapid Transit (BRT) system. These glass and steel cylinders were designed to allow passengers to pay their fare before entering, speeding up the boarding process significantly. The floors are elevated to the exact height of the bus steps, providing seamless accessibility for all passengers. This 'surface subway' design has been studied and replicated by urban planners in cities all over the world. They represent the city's long-standing commitment to efficient public transportation and innovative industrial design.

  9. 09

    Castelo do Batel

    Avenida do Batel, 1323, Batel, Curitiba, PR, 80420-090, Brazil

    The Castelo do Batel is a palatial residence inspired by the grand chateaus of the Loire Valley in France. Built by a wealthy coffee baron, it features a grey stone facade, steep roofs, and elegant turrets that stand out in the upscale Batel district. The interior is equally lavish, with imported materials and decorative arts that reflect the European tastes of the early 20th-century elite. It has been meticulously preserved and now serves as a premier venue for high-end weddings and corporate events. The castle is surrounded by manicured gardens that provide a touch of old-world charm to Curitiba’s modern urban landscape.

  10. 10

    Cathedral Basilica of Curitiba

    Praça Tiradentes, s/n, Centro, Curitiba, PR, 80020-100, Brazil

    The Cathedral Basilica of Our Lady of Light is the mother church of Curitiba and sits at the city's historical 'ground zero.' Built in a Neo-Gothic style, it was inspired by the Cathedral of Barcelona and features tall, slender towers and pointed arches. The interior is decorated with beautiful stained glass windows and religious murals that were restored to their original brilliance in recent years. As a central landmark of Praça Tiradentes, it has witnessed the city's growth from a small village to a major metropolis. It remains a focal point for both religious life and the historical identity of the Curitiban people.

  11. 11

    Federal University of Paraná (Historic Building)

    Praça Santos Andrade, s/n, Centro, Curitiba, PR, 80020-300, Brazil

    This monumental Neoclassical building is the historic seat of the first university established in Brazil. Its massive white columns and grand pediment overlook Praça Santos Andrade, creating one of the city's most formal urban vistas. The building is a symbol of Curitiba's intellectual heritage and its long-standing reputation as an educational hub. At night, the facade is beautifully illuminated, highlighting the architectural details that define the city's classical era. Today, it continues to house the university's law school and remains a central site for cultural ceremonies and student activism.

  12. 12

    Mercado Municipal de Curitiba

    Avenida Sete de Setembro, 1865, Centro, Curitiba, PR, 80060-070, Brazil

    The Municipal Market is Curitiba's premier gastronomic destination, famous for its incredible variety of exotic fruits and gourmet ingredients. It was the first market in Brazil to have a dedicated section for organic products, reflecting the city's health-conscious culture. The building’s Modernist facade houses a labyrinth of stalls where visitors can sample traditional snacks like salt-cod pastries and craft beers. It serves as a meeting point for the city’s various immigrant communities, offering everything from Japanese teas to German sausages. On weekends, the market becomes a vibrant social hub where locals shop for the freshest local produce and regional delicacies.

    iPlace Apple Premium Partner (ParkShoppingBarigui)

    ParkShoppingBarigui, Rua Professor Pedro Viriato Parigot de Souza, 600, Mossunguê, Curitiba, PR, 81200-100, Brazil

    The iPlace store at ParkShoppingBarigui is the flagship Apple Premium Partner location for the southern region of Brazil. The store follows the strict global design guidelines of Apple, emphasizing a seamless, high-tech shopping environment. It features massive glass panels, signature light oak wooden tables, and a dedicated space for customer training and technical support. The layout is designed to be open and inviting, allowing customers to interact freely with the latest hardware and ecosystem. It serves as the primary hub for technology enthusiasts in Curitiba, offering a retail experience that mirrors the world's most famous flagship stores.
