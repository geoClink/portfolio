The Tiger City

Oslo architecture

Oslo is the capital of Norway and one of the world's most livable cities, consistently ranking at the top of quality-of-life indices. The Nobel Peace Prize has been awarded in Oslo every year since 1901 — a deliberate choice by Alfred Nobel to distinguish it from the scientific prizes awarded in Stockholm. The city's fjord location gives it exceptional access to nature: in winter, locals ski to work on trails that begin at the edge of the city, and in summer the fjord fills with recreational boats and swimmers.

Before you go

  • Fly intoOslo Gardermoen Airport (OSL)

    Oslo Gardermoen Airport is Norway's main international gateway, located about 50km north of the city center. The Flytoget Airport Express train connects the airport to Oslo Central Station in just 20 minutes and is one of Scandinavia's most reliable airport rail links. The terminal is celebrated for its efficient Scandinavian design and consistently high passenger satisfaction scores.

  • CurrencyNorwegian Krone (kr)
  • LanguageNorwegian

    Norway has two official written standards: Bokmål (used by about 85% of the population) and Nynorsk. Both are taught in schools. The spoken Norwegian dialects vary significantly across the country, but Oslo's dialect (Eastern Norwegian) is most commonly heard in media and considered a neutral standard. Norwegians have extraordinarily high English proficiency — communication in English is completely seamless throughout Oslo. Norwegian is closely related to Danish and Swedish, and speakers of these languages can generally understand each other.

  • Getting aroundT-bane & Trams

    Oslo's T-bane (metro) has six lines that converge in the city center and fan out to the suburbs. The Ruter transit authority integrates the metro, trams, buses, and ferries to the Oslofjord islands under a single ticketing system. Oslo's electric trams are among the oldest continuously operating tram lines in the world. The city has invested heavily in cycling infrastructure and electric vehicles — Oslo has more EVs per capita than almost any city on Earth.
