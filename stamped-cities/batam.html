Batam architecture

Indonesia is the world's largest archipelago, stretching across more than 17,000 islands and three time zones. It is a land of incredible diversity, home to the world's largest Buddhist temple at Borobudur and over 700 distinct local languages and dialects.

      Before you go

      • Fly intoSoekarno-Hatta (CGK)

        The airport's terminals are uniquely designed with traditional Javanese 'Joglo' roofs and lush tropical gardens located between the boarding areas. The newest Terminal 3 is a massive, high-tech facility that showcases contemporary Indonesian art and serves as the hub for the national carrier, Garuda Indonesia.

      • CurrencyIndonesian Rupiah (Rp)
      • LanguageIndonesian

        Bahasa Indonesia serves as the unifying language across the thousands of islands in the archipelago. It is a straightforward language with no grammatical gender, making it accessible for visitors to learn basic phrases.

      • Getting aroundTransJakarta & MRT

        TransJakarta is the world's longest Bus Rapid Transit system and utilizes dedicated lanes to avoid the city's heavy traffic. The new Jakarta MRT provides a fast, modern underground alternative for the city's main north-south corridor. The Railink airport train offers a premium, air-conditioned connection between Soekarno-Hatta and the city center.

      13 landmarks in Batam

      1. 01

        Barelang Bridge (Tengku Fisabilillah)

        Jl. Trans Barelang, Pulau Tonton, Galang, Kota Batam, Kepulauan Riau 29481, Indonesia

        The most iconic landmark of the Riau Archipelago, this cable-stayed bridge is the first in a chain of six bridges connecting Batam, Rempang, and Galang. Conceived by B.J. Habibie, Indonesia's third president, the design features two massive pylons that dominate the coastal skyline. Its harp-like cable arrangement has earned it frequent comparisons to the Golden Gate Bridge in San Francisco. The bridge serves as a vital transportation artery while acting as a popular gathering spot for locals and tourists alike. At night, the silhouette of the structure provides a dramatic backdrop for the surrounding islands and sea.

        Batam Marriott Hotel Harbour Bay

        Harbour Bay Downtown, Jl. Duyung, Sungai Jodoh, Batu Ampar, Kota Batam, Kepulauan Riau 29432, Indonesia

        A luxury landmark on Batam's coastline, this hotel is a prime example of modern tropical hospitality architecture. It is integrated directly with the Harbour Bay international ferry terminal, providing seamless access for regional travelers. The sleek, glass-heavy facade is specifically designed to maximize panoramic views of the Singapore skyline across the strait. Inside, the architecture blends high-end international finishes with subtle nods to the local maritime environment. It stands as a symbol of Batam's evolution into a sophisticated destination for business and leisure.

      3. 03

        Great Mosque of Batam (Masjid Raya)

        Jl. Engku Putri, Tlk. Tering, Kec. Batam Kota, Kota Batam, Kepulauan Riau 29461, Indonesia

        This religious and cultural icon is famous for its unique pyramid-shaped roof rather than a traditional rounded dome. The design reflects a bold blend of modernism and traditional Malay-Islamic architectural elements. Its central prayer hall is spacious and airy, designed to facilitate natural light and ventilation through geometric openings. Located in Batam Center, it serves as a spiritual anchor for the city's administrative and commercial district. The surrounding courtyard and gardens offer a peaceful retreat from the bustling urban environment of the island.

      4. 04

        Nagoya Hill Mall

        Jl. Teuku Umar No.1, Lubuk Baja Kota, Kec. Lubuk Baja, Kota Batam, Kepulauan Riau 29444, Indonesia

        As the largest shopping complex in Batam, Nagoya Hill is a sprawling 'superblock' that combines retail, dining, and hospitality. Its architectural layout is famous for the 'Food Street,' a massive indoor-outdoor pedestrian corridor lined with eateries. The building utilizes a modern commercial aesthetic designed to handle high volumes of regional weekend foot traffic. It integrates various wings that cater to electronics, fashion, and traditional Indonesian crafts all under one roof. The mall remains a central pillar of Batam's economy and its most recognized shopping destination.

      5. 05

        Maha Vihara Duta Maitreya Monastery

        Jl. Bukit Beruntung, Sei Panas, Kec. Batam Kota, Kota Batam, Kepulauan Riau 29461, Indonesia

        This monastery is one of the largest Buddhist temples in Southeast Asia, attracting pilgrims from across the region. The sprawling complex is famous for its collection of hundreds of laughing Buddha statues in various poses. Its architecture features large, open halls with traditional Chinese motifs and vibrant red and gold decorative elements. The site functions not just as a place of worship, but also as a center for community education and vegetarianism. Visitors often comment on the serene atmosphere that contrasts with the surrounding industrial zones of Sei Panas.

      6. 06

        Masjid Tanwirun Naja (Tanjak Mosque)

        Kawasan Bandara Hang Nadim, Batu Besar, Kec. Nongsa, Kota Batam, Kepulauan Riau 29466, Indonesia

        This striking new addition to Batam's skyline is located conveniently within the Hang Nadim Airport complex. The mosque is uniquely shaped like a 'Tanjak,' which is a traditional Malay male headgear signifying honor and authority. Its neo-vernacular architecture uses modern materials to interpret ancient cultural symbols in a contemporary way. The blue and gold color palette of the roof serves as a vibrant welcoming signal to travelers arriving by air. It has quickly become a pride of the Riau Archipelago for its successful integration of ethnic identity and modern design.

        One Residence Batam

        Jl. Engku Putri, Tlk. Tering, Kec. Batam Kota, Kota Batam, Kepulauan Riau 29461, Indonesia

        One Residence stands as one of the most prominent luxury residential towers in the Batam Center district. Its sleek, glass-wrapped architectural style reflects the rapid urbanization and economic growth of the city. The tower is strategically positioned at the edge of the coast to provide residents with panoramic views of the ocean. From its upper floors, one can clearly see the 'Welcome to Batam' sign and the distant skyscrapers of Singapore. The building represents the new standard for high-end vertical living in the Riau Archipelago region.

      8. 08

        Galang Refugee Hospital (Historical Site)

        Pulau Galang, Sijantung, Kec. Galang, Kota Batam, Kepulauan Riau 29485, Indonesia

        This site holds immense historical and humanitarian significance as a former sanctuary for Vietnamese refugees. Built originally by the UN and the Indonesian government, these barrack-style structures once housed thousands of 'Boat People.' The architecture is strictly functional and utilitarian, designed for rapid deployment and medical efficiency. Today, the site is preserved as a museum to honor the history of the Riau Archipelago as a place of international refuge. Walking through the preserved wooden wards provides a somber and educational look into the events of the late 20th century.

      9. 09

        Meisterstadt (Pollux Habibie Towers)

        Jl. Ahmad Yani, Tlk. Tering, Kec. Batam Kota, Kota Batam, Kepulauan Riau 29461, Indonesia

        Meisterstadt is a massive mega-project conceptualized as a sustainable 'city within a city.' Named after former President B.J. Habibie, this complex features the tallest residential buildings in Batam. The architectural design utilizes a vertical urbanism approach to maximize space in the heart of the city. The towers are intended to transform Batam into a world-class financial and medical hub that rivals its regional neighbors. Its high-tech glass facades and integrated infrastructure reflect a vision of Indonesia's future industrial and urban potential.

      10. 10

        Vihara Budhi Bhakti (Tua Pek Kong)

        Jl. Pembangunan, Lubuk Baja Kota, Kec. Lubuk Baja, Kota Batam, Kepulauan Riau 29444, Indonesia

        This is the oldest Chinese temple in Batam, located in the historic heart of the Nagoya district. Its architecture is a vibrant example of Southern Chinese temple design, complete with ornate dragon-entwined pillars. The central pagoda and meticulously carved altars serve as a spiritual anchor for the local Chinese-Indonesian community. It stands in stark contrast to the modern shopping malls and glass towers that have grown up around it. The temple grounds remain a popular site for both daily worship and major lunar New Year celebrations.

        Hang Nadim Terminal 2

        Jl. Hang Nadim, Batu Besar, Kec. Nongsa, Kota Batam, Kepulauan Riau 29466, Indonesia

        This project represents a major modernization of Batam's gateway to handle increasing international transit traffic. Terminal 2 features a sleek, aerodynamic roofline that symbolizes the island's role as a logistics and aviation hub. The architecture focuses on airy, high-ceilinged spaces that utilize glass walls to flood the interior with natural light. It blends industrial efficiency with modern aesthetic appeal to provide a comfortable experience for global travelers. The expansion is a key component of the city's strategy to become a premier regional transport destination.

      12. 12

        Kantor Walikota Batam (City Hall)

        Jl. Engku Putri No.1, Tlk. Tering, Kec. Batam Kota, Kota Batam, Kepulauan Riau 29461, Indonesia

        The administrative heart of the city, this building is a classic example of Indonesian civic architecture from the late 20th century. It features a prominent tiered roof and wide, symmetrical wings that overlook the Engku Putri central plain. The structure stands as a symbol of Batam's rapid transition from a remote outpost to a vital industrial municipality. Its design balances traditional Indonesian roof forms with the functional requirements of a modern government office. It remains the site of major civic ceremonies and the primary executive hub for the island's development.

        Apple Premium Partner (Grand Batam Mall)

        Grand Batam Mall, Level 1, Jl. Pembangunan, Selaya, Lubuk Baja, Kota Batam, Kepulauan Riau 29444, Indonesia

        Located within the premier Grand Batam Mall, this flagship-level partner store adheres to Apple's strict global design language. The architecture features minimalist white surfaces, light wood 'Avenue' displays, and expansive open-plan consultation areas. It serves as the primary hub for authorized Apple products and support within the Riau Archipelago. The clean, brightly lit interior reflects the growing demand for luxury retail experiences in Batam's downtown core. This space demonstrates how international retail standards are being integrated into the island's modern commercial landscape.
