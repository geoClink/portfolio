How it works

Save. Drive. Stamp.

  1. Save the schools

    Tap the heart on any campus to add it to My College List. Find schools by nickname, like "UGA" or "Ole Miss".

  2. Plan the road trip

    One tap lines them up: a day at each school, in a short driving order, with the miles between them.

  3. Visit and stamp

    Walk each campus's landmarks, do its traditions, and collect a stamp in school colors.

Plan · My College List + College Road Trip

From a short list to a road trip.

Most families visit several schools in one trip. Stamped! keeps the list, works out the order, and gives you one plan to follow.

  • A day at each school, sorted into a short driving order
  • Find hotels near campus for each night of the trip
  • Trips and your college list sync over iCloud between iPhone and iPad

Visit · Campus maps in 3D

Know the campus before you park.

Fly over each campus in photoreal 3D, then use Plan a Campus Visit for a one-day walking tour of its landmark buildings, with the walking time between stops.

  • The landmark buildings on every campus, with the story behind each one
  • Food, coffee and places to stay on the campus map
  • A 10-question architecture quiz for every campus

Do · Campus traditions

Rub the statue. Ring the bell.

Over 250 traditions students actually do, from lucky statues to bells and famous gates. Check them off as you go and earn the Traditions Keeper badge. It gives a long day of tours something to look forward to.

  • Each tradition on the map, with what it is and why students do it
  • Campus stamps in school colors
  • Achievements for stamping every campus in a conference
